    WHEN TO TREK IN NEPAL

    Seasonal Weather & Timing Matrix

    Nepal offers world-class trekking year-round. Select a season below to find out which region matches your calendar.

    Peak Season Clarity

    Crystal-clear blue skies, crisp air, and ideal daytime temperatures (15°C – 20°C). Post-monsoon washed air offers unmatched mountain photography.

    Recommended Regions

    All Regions Prime: Everest Base Camp, Annapurna Circuit, Manaslu Circuit, Kanchenjunga, and Langtang Valley.

    Clothing Strategy

    Warm down jacket for chilly evenings, mid-weight fleece layers, sun protection for high altitude, and rated sleeping bag.

    Blooms & Warmth

    Rhododendron forests in full crimson bloom between 2,000m – 3,500m. Longer daylight hours and warming temperatures.

    Recommended Regions

    Annapurna Sanctuary, Poon Hill, Langtang Valley, Everest Gokyo Lakes, and Makalu Barun.

    Clothing Strategy

    Breathable moisture-wicking tees for sunny days, windproof jacket for mountain passes, and rain shell layer for light showers.

    Rain-Shadow Magic

    While lower valleys experience monsoon rains, trans-Himalayan rain-shadow zones behind main peaks stay dry and sunny!

    Recommended Regions

    Upper Mustang Walled Kingdom & Upper Dolpo Wilderness. Experience green mountain pastures and zero rain!

    Clothing Strategy

    Dust protection (buff, sunglasses) for dry desert plateau winds, lightweight sun hoodies, and sturdy sand/rock boots.

    Quiet Low-Altitude Trails

    Crisp sunny days with zero trail crowds. High passes (Thorong La, Larkya La) may close due to snow, but mid-altitude treks shine.

    Recommended Regions

    Ghorepani Poon Hill, Mardi Himal Ridge, Everest to Tengboche, and Lower Langtang.

    Clothing Strategy

    Heavyweight down jacket (-20°C rating), thermal base layers, microspikes for icy trail sections, and insulated gloves.
