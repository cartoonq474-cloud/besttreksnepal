Comprehensive Day-by-Day Schedule

14-Day Detailed Itinerary

Day 01

Fly Kathmandu / Ramechhap to Lukla (2,846m) & Trek to Phakding

2,610m 4 hrs / 8 km

Early morning transfer to domestic airport for the thrilling 35-minute flight to Lukla (2,846m), soaring over alpine valleys with panoramic Himalayan peaks. Upon landing, meet your dedicated Sherpa porter team. After a short tea break, begin trekking along the gentle downhill trail following the milky glacial waters of the Dudh Koshi River through Chhuthawa to reach Phakding village for your first teahouse overnight stay.

✓ Overnight: Standard Teahouse Lodge in Phakding (Twin Room) | Meals: Lunch, Dinner
Day 02

Trek Phakding to Namche Bazaar via Hillary Suspension Bridge

3,440m 6 hrs / 11 km

Cross several prayer-flag-draped suspension bridges over the Dudh Koshi River, passing through Monjo and entering the official checkpoint of Sagarmatha National Park. After crossing the dizzying Hillary Suspension Bridge suspended high above the gorge, begin the steady, iconic ascent through fragrant pine forests. Catch your very first view of Mt. Everest peeking between Lhotse and Nuptse before emerging into the vibrant natural amphitheater of Namche Bazaar.

✓ Overnight: Comfortable Lodge in Namche Bazaar | Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner
Day 03

Acclimatization Day in Namche: Hike to Hotel Everest View (3,880m)

3,880m 4 hrs Active Rest

Mandatory acclimatization day crucial for altitude adaptation. Embark on a morning hike ascending past the Syangboche airstrip to the famous Hotel Everest View (3,880m) — one of the highest luxury hotels in the world. Sip hot coffee on the terrace while marveling at unhindered vistas of Mt. Everest, Ama Dablam, Thamserku, and Nuptse. Continue to the traditional Sherpa villages of Khumjung (visiting the school founded by Sir Hillary) before returning to Namche for afternoon bakeries and market exploration.

✓ Overnight: Comfortable Lodge in Namche Bazaar | Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner
Day 04

Trek Namche Bazaar to Tengboche Monastery (3,867m)

3,867m 5 hrs / 10 km

Traverse along high, scenic cliff-side contours overlooking the Dudh Koshi canyon with panoramic vistas of Everest, Lhotse, and Ama Dablam rising majestically ahead. Descend steeply through rhododendron forests to the riverside outpost of Phunki Tenga for lunch. Climb the winding switchbacks through fragrant juniper woods to reach Tengboche, perched on a dramatic mountain ridge. Visit Tengboche Monastery, the spiritual hub of the Khumbu, and witness evening Buddhist prayers.

✓ Overnight: Panoramic Teahouse in Tengboche | Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner
Day 05

Trek Tengboche to Dingboche (4,410m) via Pangboche

4,410m 5.5 hrs / 11 km

Descend through birch, conifer, and rhododendron forests to Deboche, crossing the Imja Khola on a suspension bridge. Hike past intricately carved Mani stone walls to the ancient village of Pangboche, home to the oldest monastery in the Everest region. As you climb past the tree line into alpine shrub terrain, enter the broad Imja Valley and arrive at the picturesque stone-walled village of Dingboche, known as the "Summer Valley".

✓ Overnight: Alpine Lodge in Dingboche | Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner
Day 06

Acclimatization Day in Dingboche: Climb Nangkartshang Peak (5,083m)

5,083m 4-5 hrs Active Rest

Our second vital acclimatization day prepares your body for the 5,000m+ push ahead. Undertake a steep climb up the ridge behind Dingboche to Nangkartshang Peak (5,083m). From the summit stupa, enjoy panoramic views of Makalu (8,485m — 5th highest), Lhotse, Cho Oyu, Ama Dablam, and the massive Imja Glacier. Descend to Dingboche for a restful afternoon and hydration.

✓ Overnight: Alpine Lodge in Dingboche | Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner
Day 07

Trek Dingboche to Lobuche via Thokla Pass Memorials

4,940m 5 hrs / 8 km

Trek across wide alpine tundra to Dughla (Thokla) for hot tea before making the steep 1-hour climb up Thokla Pass (4,830m). At the crest, spend solemn moments among the stone chortens and memorials dedicated to fallen Everest climbers and brave Sherpas (including Scott Fischer and Babu Chiri Sherpa). Continue alongside the lateral moraine of the Khumbu Glacier to the mountain outpost of Lobuche.

✓ Overnight: High Altitude Lodge in Lobuche | Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner
Day 08

Lobuche to Gorak Shep (5,164m) & Trek to Everest Base Camp (5,364m)!

5,364m 7-8 hrs / 13 km

The pinnacle day of your Himalayan journey! Trek through rocky glacial moraines and undulating boulder paths to Gorak Shep (5,164m), the world's highest permanent settlement. Check into the lodge, deposit heavy daypacks, and push onward toward Everest Base Camp (5,364m). Walk across the Khumbu Glacier, hear the deep echoes of ice shifts, and stand beside the iconic spray-painted EBC rock milestone beneath the tumbling seracs of the Khumbu Icefall. Return to Gorak Shep for overnight.

✓ Overnight: Gorak Shep Lodge | Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner
Day 09

Summit Kala Patthar (5,545m) for Sunrise & Descend to Pheriche

5,545m Summit 7 hrs / 15 km

Pre-dawn ascent (4:30 AM) with headlamps climbing the steep slopes of Kala Patthar (5,545m). Reach the prayer-flag-draped summit just as the morning sun casts blazing golden light directly onto Mt. Everest, Nuptse, Changtse, and the South Col. Gaze 360 degrees across the greatest amphitheater of 8,000m giants on Earth. Descend to Gorak Shep for hot breakfast, then hike down to the lower, oxygen-rich valley of Pheriche (4,240m).

✓ Overnight: Pheriche Himalayan Lodge | Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner
Day 10

Trek Pheriche to Namche Bazaar via Tengboche

3,440m 6-7 hrs / 14 km

With abundant oxygen filling your lungs, descending feels effortless. Retrace steps past Pangboche and re-enter lush rhododendron forests. Climb back through Tengboche, descend to Phunki Tenga, and traverse back along the scenic cliffs into Namche Bazaar. Celebrate your base camp triumph with warm showers, German bakeries, artisanal espresso, and live Sherpa pub music.

✓ Overnight: Comfortable Lodge in Namche Bazaar | Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner
Day 11

Trek Namche Bazaar to Lukla via Hillary Bridge

2,846m 7 hrs / 19 km

Final trekking day. Descend steeply through pine forests, cross the Hillary Suspension Bridge, and exit Sagarmatha National Park at Monjo. Continue along the Dudh Koshi via Phakding and Ghat, finishing with a celebratory final climb up into Lukla. Host a farewell celebration dinner with your Sherpa guides and porters to thank them for their extraordinary dedication.

✓ Overnight: Lukla Mountain Resort Lodge | Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner
Day 12

Morning Flight from Lukla to Kathmandu / Ramechhap

1,400m 35 min flight

Board an early morning flight from Lukla back to Kathmandu (or Ramechhap with private vehicle transfer). Transfer to your hotel in Thamel. Enjoy a long hot shower, laundry service, and a celebratory evening in Kathmandu.

✓ Overnight: 4-Star Hotel in Thamel, Kathmandu | Meals: Breakfast
Day 13

Buffer / Contingency Day in Kathmandu Valley

1,400m Full Day

Essential built-in buffer day in case of weather delays for Lukla flights. If on schedule, take an optional guided heritage tour of UNESCO World Heritage sites including Swayambhunath (Monkey Temple), Boudhanath Stupa, and Pashupatinath Temple, or relax with a traditional Ayurvedic massage.

✓ Overnight: 4-Star Hotel in Thamel, Kathmandu | Meals: Breakfast, Farewell Dinner
Day 14

Final Departure / International Airport Transfer

1,400m Airport Transfer

Our private airport representative and driver will transfer you to Tribhuvan International Airport (TIA) 3 hours prior to your scheduled flight home. Depart with lifelong memories of standing in the shadow of the world's greatest mountain.

Accommodation for the Everest Base Camp Trek

During the trek, you will stay in mountain teahouse lodges run by local Sherpa families. Rooms are private twin-share bedrooms equipped with wooden beds, foam mattresses, clean bedsheets, and pillows. In Kathmandu, you will stay at a premium 4-star hotel (e.g., Hotel Mulberry / Fairfield by Marriott) with private en-suite bathroom and buffet breakfast.

At lower elevations (Lukla, Phakding, Namche Bazaar), private en-suite bathrooms and electrical charging inside the room are frequently available. Above 4,000m (Dingboche, Lobuche, Gorak Shep), lodges become more rustic with clean communal Western-style or squat toilets.

💡 Sleeping Comfort Tip: While blankets are provided by lodges, rooms are unheated. We provide a complimentary sanitized -20°C alpine down sleeping bag for maximum night warmth.

Available Food in Everest Base Camp Trek

All meals (13 Breakfasts, 11 Lunches, 11 Dinners) on the trail are included in your package. You can order fresh dishes directly from the extensive teahouse menus prepared by trained lodge cooks.

Popular Menu Options:

  • Breakfast: Oatmeal porridge, muesli with hot milk, Tibetan bread with honey, eggs (fried, boiled, scrambled, omelets), hash browns, pancakes, and ginger lemon honey tea.
  • Lunch & Dinner: Traditional Nepali Dal Bhat Power 24 Hour (lentil soup, rice, fresh vegetable curry, spinach, and papadum with free refills!), Sherpa potato stew (Shakpa), momo dumplings, fried noodles (Chowmein), thukpa noodle soup, pasta, pizza, and garlic soup.
🥗 Health Recommendation: We strongly advise sticking to a vegetarian diet above Namche Bazaar (3,440m) because all meat is carried up on foot without refrigeration. Garlic soup is served every evening as a natural altitude aid.

Kathmandu to Lukla Flight Details & Alternatives

The flight from Kathmandu (or Manthali Airport in Ramechhap during peak seasons) to Tenzing-Hillary Airport in Lukla (2,846m) is a world-renowned 25-minute scenic twin-otter mountain flight offering panoramic views of the central and eastern Himalayas.

Peak Season Ramechhap Rerouting: During high trekking seasons (March–May and October–November), Civil Aviation Authority of Nepal reroutes Lukla flights via Ramechhap (Manthali Airport) to avoid Kathmandu airspace traffic. We provide comfortable private tourist transport from Kathmandu to Ramechhap (approx. 4 hours) included in your package.

🚁 Helicopter Upgrade Option: To avoid driving to Ramechhap or during adverse weather delays, private or shared helicopter flights direct from Kathmandu to Lukla are available upon request.

Everest Base Camp Trek Difficulty & Fitness Requirements

The Everest Base Camp Trek is graded Grade 4 (Moderate to Strenuous). No technical climbing, ropes, or mountaineering skills are required. However, you will walk 5 to 7 hours per day across 130 km of alpine terrain reaching a maximum altitude of 5,545m at Kala Patthar.

Recommended Training: 8 to 12 weeks of cardiovascular training (jogging, stair climbing, cycling, hill walking with a 5kg daypack). Mental stamina and steady, slow breathing ("Bistari Bistari") are the secrets to success.

🏆 98.4% Success Rate: Our 14-day schedule incorporates 2 full acclimatization rest days in Namche and Dingboche, giving our trekkers an industry-leading summit rate.

Best Time & Seasons for Everest Base Camp Trek

There are two prime trekking seasons in the Khumbu Himalayas:

  • Spring (March to May): Warm days (15°C to 20°C in valleys), blooming pink and red rhododendron forests, crystal clear mornings, and vibrant climbing expeditions setting up tents at Base Camp.
  • Autumn (September to November): The most popular season with stable weather, ultra-clear post-monsoon mountain visibility, mild daytime temperatures, and deep blue skies.
❄️ Winter & Monsoon: Winter (Dec–Feb) is dry and quiet with crisp skies but freezing nights (-15°C). Summer/Monsoon (Jun–Aug) brings heavy rain, low cloud cover, and flight disruptions.

Altitude Sickness, Symptoms & Acclimatization Safety

Acute Mountain Sickness (AMS) can affect anyone above 2,500m regardless of fitness level. Symptoms include mild headache, nausea, dizziness, loss of appetite, and difficulty sleeping.

Our Safety Protocol:

  • Twice-daily pulse oximeter blood oxygen saturation (SpO2) and heart rate monitoring.
  • Portable emergency medical oxygen tank and comprehensive first-aid kit carried on all departures.
  • Drink 3 to 4 liters of clean water daily and avoid alcohol and smoking completely.
  • Diamox (Acetazolamide) 125mg–250mg can be used as a preventative aid after consulting your doctor.
🚨 Emergency Rule: If severe symptoms persist, our certified Sherpa guides immediately coordinate descent to a lower elevation and dispatch 24/7 helicopter evacuation if necessary.

Drinking Water, Hot Showers & Electricity on the Trail

Drinking Water: To eliminate single-use plastic pollution in Sagarmatha National Park, bottled water is restricted in upper regions. You can purchase boiled filtered water at lodges ($1.50–$3 per liter) or fill tap water and treat it with Aquatabs / Katadyn water filter.

Hot Showers: Available at most teahouses powered by gas geysers or solar heating ($3 to $5 per shower). Above Dingboche (4,410m), we recommend wet wipes to prevent catching chills.

Battery Charging: Solar/hydro outlets are available in teahouse dining rooms ($2–$4 per device/power bank). Bring a 20,000mAh power bank.

Internet, Wi-Fi & Mobile SIM Card Connectivity

Mobile 4G Data: Ncell and Nepal Telecom (NTC) offer 4G mobile reception up to Namche Bazaar, Tengboche, and Pangboche. We assist you in obtaining a tourist SIM card in Kathmandu ($10 for 20GB data).

Everest Link Satellite Wi-Fi: High-speed prepaid Wi-Fi cards (Everest Link) can be purchased at all teahouses across the Khumbu region ($5 for 10GB / $20 for 30GB usable across the entire trek route).

Money, ATMs & Trail Expense Budgeting (Cash in NPR)

There are no reliable ATMs above Namche Bazaar (3,440m). While Namche has several ATM machines, they occasionally run out of cash due to power or satellite outages.

Recommended Cash to Bring: We recommend exchanging and carrying approximately $200 to $250 USD in Nepali Rupees (NPR) cash per person for personal trail expenses (hot showers, charging, Wi-Fi cards, snacks, hot bakery treats, and guide/porter tipping).

Nepal is widely recognized as one of the safest destinations in the world for solo female travelers. On all our Everest Base Camp departures, solo female trekkers are assigned private single rooms (subject to seasonal lodge capacity) or paired exclusively with other female travelers.

Our licensed Sherpa guides adhere strictly to international traveler safeguarding standards. Female guide requests can also be arranged upon advance notice.

We are strict signatories of the International Porter Protection Group (IPPG) guidelines. Every porter on our expeditions carries a maximum load of 15kg per trekker (total 30kg for 2 trekkers), receives above-market wages, warm mountain footwear and jackets, full emergency medical insurance, and proper teahouse shelter.

Tipping Guideline: While discretionary, a standard tip of $120–$150 USD per trekker is customarily pooled at the end of the trek in Lukla and split between your guide and porter.

Acclimatization Rest Days: Namche Bazaar & Dingboche Strategy

Our 14-day schedule incorporates two active acclimatization rest days following the golden rule of mountaineering: "Climb High, Sleep Low".

  • Day 4 (Namche Bazaar - 3,440m): Hike to Everest View Hotel (3,880m) for your first panoramic sight of Mt. Everest, Ama Dablam, and Thamserku, then descend back to Namche to sleep.
  • Day 7 (Dingboche - 4,410m): Climb the ridge of Nagarjun Hill (5,100m) with incredible vistas of Makalu (8,481m) and Island Peak before descending to sleep.

Everest Base Camp (5,364m) vs Kala Patthar (5,545m) Comparison

Everest Base Camp (5,364m): Located on the moving Khumbu Glacier directly beneath the tumbling Khumbu Icefall. You stand where summit expeditions stage their climbs. Note: Mt. Everest’s actual peak is partially obscured by the massive West Shoulder and Nuptse ridge when standing at Base Camp itself.

Kala Patthar (5,545m): The absolute visual climax of the trek! Ascended on Day 10 at sunrise, this high black ridge provides the closest, most dramatic, unobstructed panoramic view of Mt. Everest’s summit pyramid, Lhotse, Nuptse, and Pumori glowing in golden morning sunlight.

Himalayan Rescue Association (HRA) Clinics & Emergency Doctors

The Khumbu region features world-class high-altitude medical outposts operated by the non-profit Himalayan Rescue Association (HRA) at Pheriche (4,280m) and Everest Base Camp (Everest ER).

Staffed by international and Nepali mountain medicine physicians, they conduct daily high-altitude safety seminars and provide hyperbaric Gamow bag treatments, oxygen therapy, and emergency triage for all trekkers.

Frequently Asked Questions: Everest Base Camp

Everything you need to know regarding Lukla flight logistics, altitude sickness protocols, permits, teahouse life, and booking terms.

Lukla Flights

How do Lukla flights operate and what about Ramechhap (Manthali) transfers?

During peak trekking months (Spring: March–May, Autumn: October–November), the Civil Aviation Authority of Nepal (CAAN) routes Lukla flights through Manthali Airport in Ramechhap (4-hour scenic drive from Kathmandu) to avoid air-traffic congestion at Kathmandu's international runway. In off-peak months, flights operate directly from Kathmandu.

All-Inclusive Transfer: Best Treks Nepal provides private vehicle transportation between Kathmandu and Ramechhap, airport assistance, and guaranteed flight manifests included in your package.
Altitude Safety

How does your 14-day schedule prevent Acute Mountain Sickness (AMS)?

Our itinerary adheres to the golden rule of high-altitude mountaineering: climb high, sleep low. We spend two nights at Namche Bazaar (3,440m) and two nights at Dingboche (4,410m) with mandatory active acclimatization day-hikes (Hotel Everest View 3,880m and Nangkartshang Peak 5,083m).

Active Medical Monitoring: Our licensed Sherpa guides conduct twice-daily blood oxygen saturation and heart-rate checks using clinical pulse oximeters. Supplemental bottled oxygen and 24/7 helicopter evacuation standby are maintained at all times.
Permits & Fees

What permits are required for Everest Base Camp and are they included?

Two mandatory permits are required for all foreign trekkers entering the Khumbu region:

  • Sagarmatha National Park Entry Permit (SNPP): NPR 3,000 + 13% VAT (~$26 USD)
  • Khumbu Pasang Lhamu Rural Municipality Entry Permit: NPR 3,000 (~$23 USD)
Zero Paperwork: Both permits are 100% arranged and paid for by Best Treks Nepal before departure and are fully included in the $1,350 package price.
Lodges & Food

What are teahouses like and what food is available on the trek?

Accommodations consist of clean, twin-share wooden rooms with comfortable foam mattresses, pillows, and warm blankets. Dining halls are heated every evening by central cast-iron stoves. Menus feature hygienic, carbohydrate-rich meals including authentic Nepali Dal Bhat (unlimited refills), noodle soups, fried rice, pasta, momos, eggs, and porridge.

Power & Wi-Fi: Solar charging for phones and cameras is available in all dining halls ($2–$4). Everest Link satellite Wi-Fi cards and local NTC/Ncell 4G data work across most villages.
Physical Fitness

How fit do I need to be for the 14-day EBC trek?

You do not need previous mountaineering or technical climbing experience. However, good cardiovascular fitness is recommended, as you will be walking 5 to 7 hours per day over rocky, uphill terrain with a light daypack (4–6kg). We recommend 8–12 weeks of aerobic preparation (hiking, stair climbing, running, and cycling) prior to arrival.

Insurance

What travel insurance policy is required for Everest Base Camp?

All trekkers must hold valid travel insurance that explicitly covers high-altitude trekking up to 6,000m (20,000 ft) and emergency helicopter evacuation & hospital inpatient care.

Recommended Providers: World Nomads (Explorer Plan), Global Rescue, Ripcord Rescue Travel Insurance, and Allianz Global Assistance.
