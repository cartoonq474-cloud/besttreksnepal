Wilderness of the Black Giant

Makalu Base Camp Trek (19 Days)

The Makalu Base Camp Trek (19 Days) takes you into one of the most untouched and biodiverse wilderness regions in the Himalayas: Makalu-Barun National Park. Dominated by the soaring black granite pyramid of Mt. Makalu (8,485m)—the world’s fifth-highest peak—this expedition combines rugged alpine passes, vertical granite gorges, and pristine high-altitude glaciers.

Starting with a scenic flight from Kathmandu to Tumlingtar and a rugged 4WD drive to Num, the route ascends through Rai and Sherpa villages to cross the legendary Shipton La Pass (4,216m) and Keke La (4,170m). Descending into the sheer, untouched Barun Valley gorge, you trek past towering waterfalls and hanging glaciers to reach Makalu Base Camp (4,870m), with views of Mt. Everest’s rare Kangshung Face, Lhotse, Chamlang, and Baruntse.

Makalu Base Camp (4,870m)

Shipton La & Keke La Passes (4,216m)

Pristine Barun River Gorge

Trek through vertical granite cliffs, hanging glaciers, alpine meadows, and waterfalls in Yangle Kharka.

Tashi Gaon Sherpa Culture

Experience traditional Sherpa and Rai hospitality in isolated hill settlements with stone monasteries.

Rare Wildlife Sanctuary

Home to the elusive Red Panda, Snow Leopard, Himalayan Black Bear, and over 400 species of birds.

Everest Kangshung Face Views

Ascend the high ridge above Makalu Base Camp (5,200m) to view Everest's rare eastern Kangshung wall and Lhotse.

19-Day Expedition Blueprint

19-Day Makalu Base Camp Detailed Itinerary

Complete route from Tumlingtar through Num, Tashi Gaon, Shipton La Pass (4,216m), the Barun Valley, and Makalu Base Camp (4,870m).

Day 1 Flight Kathmandu to Tumlingtar (518m) & 4WD Drive to Num (1,560m)
1,560m 40m Flight + 4.5 hrs Drive +

Take a morning flight from Kathmandu to Tumlingtar in the Arun Valley. Meet your private 4WD driver and drive along ridge roads through Khandbari and Chichila to reach the village of Num (1,560m) overlooking the Arun River.

Day 2 Trek Num to Seduwa (1,500m) via Arun River Valley
1,500m 5 hrs / 10 km +

Descend steeply through terraced fields and bamboo thickets to the suspension bridge over the roaring Arun River (770m). Climb up through forest and cardamom fields to the ridge village of Seduwa (1,500m) and enter Makalu-Barun National Park.

Day 3 Trek Seduwa to Tashi Gaon (2,100m) through Sherpa Farmlands
2,100m 5 hrs / 9 km +

Walk along quiet stone staircases through the villages of Mulgaon and terraced crop fields. Climb steadily to Tashi Gaon (2,100m), the last permanent Sherpa settlement before the high wilderness.

Day 4 Trek Tashi Gaon to Khongma Danda (3,500m)
3,500m 6 hrs / 8 km +

Steep climb through dense forests of oak, rhododendron, and fir. Cross stone steps and moss-draped ridges through Chipla to reach the open crest of Khongma Danda (3,500m).

Day 5 Acclimatization & Rest Day at Khongma Danda (3,500m)
3,500m Acclimatization Day +

Essential acclimatization day before crossing the high pass. Take a side hike along the ridge towards Kauma viewpoint (3,800m) for panorama views of Chamlang, Peak 6, and Kanchenjunga.

Day 6 🏔️ Cross Shipton La (4,216m) & Keke La (4,170m) to Dobate (3,540m)
Shipton La 4,216m 7 hrs / 10 km +

Magnificent pass crossing day. Climb over Ghungru La (4,050m), past the sacred lake of Kalo Pokhari, and over Shipton La (4,216m) and Keke La (4,170m). Descend through rhododendron forests to Dobate / Mumbuk (3,540m).

Day 7 Trek Dobate to Yangle Kharka (3,557m) along Barun River Gorge
3,557m 6 hrs / 11 km +

Descend into the sheer Barun River canyon. Follow the raging river through birch and pine woodlands past vertical granite walls and roaring waterfalls to the open meadow of Yangle Kharka (3,557m).

Day 8 Trek Yangle Kharka to Langmale Kharka / Merek (4,410m)
4,410m 5.5 hrs / 10 km +

Ascend the widening glacial valley passing Nghe Kharka and Ripok. View the sheer rock walls of Peak 4 and Peak 6, climbing through alpine juniper scrub to Langmale Kharka (4,410m).

Day 9 🏔️ Trek Langmale Kharka to Makalu Base Camp (4,870m)
Makalu BC 4,870m 5 hrs / 8 km +

Climb along the lateral moraine of the Barun Glacier past Shershon. Emerge into the amphitheater of Makalu Base Camp (4,870m) directly beneath the sheer South Face of Mt. Makalu (8,485m) and Baruntse.

Day 10 Exploration of Makalu Base Camp & High Ridge Viewpoint (5,200m)
5,200m Viewpoint Exploration Day +

Day to explore the Barun Glacier and climb the ridge north of Base Camp (5,200m). Gaze at the rare eastern Kangshung Face of Mt. Everest (8,848m), Lhotse, and Chamlang.

Day 11 Trek Makalu Base Camp to Yangle Kharka (3,557m)
3,557m 6.5 hrs / 16 km +

Descend past Shershon, Langmale Kharka, and Merek down the Barun valley to Yangle Kharka (3,557m).

Day 12 Trek Yangle Kharka to Dobate (3,540m)
3,540m 5.5 hrs / 11 km +

Follow the Barun River down the canyon and climb through rhododendron woodlands to Dobate (3,540m).

Day 13 Cross Keke La & Shipton La to Khongma Danda (3,500m)
Shipton La 4,216m 6.5 hrs / 10 km +

Cross back over Keke La (4,170m) and Shipton La (4,216m) past Kalo Pokhari lake. Descend along the ridge to Khongma Danda (3,500m).

Day 14 Trek Khongma Danda to Tashi Gaon (2,100m)
2,100m 5 hrs / 8 km +

Descend through the lush rhododendron forests and stone staircases back to the friendly Sherpa village of Tashi Gaon (2,100m).

Day 15 Trek Tashi Gaon to Seduwa (1,500m)
1,500m 4.5 hrs / 9 km +

Trek through cultivated farmlands and Rai settlements down to Seduwa (1,500m).

Day 16 Trek Seduwa to Num (1,560m) & 4WD Drive to Tumlingtar (518m)
518m 4 hrs Trek + 4 hrs Drive +

Descend to the Arun River bridge and climb up to Num. Meet your 4WD jeep crew and drive to Tumlingtar (518m).

Day 17 Buffer / Contingency Day in Tumlingtar
518m Buffer Day +

Buffer day for weather flexibility on mountain flights or trail delays. Relax in Tumlingtar or explore local tea gardens and markets.

Day 18 Flight Tumlingtar to Kathmandu (1,400m)
1,400m Flight to KTM +

Take a morning flight from Tumlingtar to Kathmandu. Transfer to your hotel for relaxation.

Day 19 Farewell Celebration in Kathmandu & Departure
1,400m Celebration +

Farewell dinner in Kathmandu with our team and presentation of your Makalu Base Camp expedition certificate.

1. Standing at Makalu Base Camp (4,870m) +

Makalu Base Camp sits in a massive amphitheater surrounded by the sheer 3,000m south face of Mt. Makalu (8,485m), Baruntse (7,129m), and the churning icefall of the Barun Glacier.

2. Crossing Shipton La (4,216m) & Keke La (4,170m) +

Named after explorer Eric Shipton, the pass connects the Arun watershed with the Barun Valley. It is crossed on Day 6 and Day 13, past the sacred turquoise alpine tarn of Kalo Pokhari.

3. Barun Valley Microclimates & Ecosystems +

The Barun Valley is a unique microclimate featuring vertical granite walls, hanging waterfalls, lush rhododendron and fir forests, and high alpine meadows with rare orchids.

4. View of Mt. Everest Kangshung Face & Lhotse +

From the high ridge above Makalu Base Camp (5,200m), trekkers are treated to the rare eastern Kangshung Face of Mt. Everest and the imposing mass of Lhotse.

5. Makalu-Barun National Park Wildlife +

The park is a global biodiversity hotspot protecting the endangered Red Panda, Snow Leopard, Musk Deer, Himalayan Black Bear, Clouded Leopard, and over 400 species of alpine birds.

6. Tashi Gaon & Rai Culture +

The lower trail winds through Rai and Sherpa settlements. Tashi Gaon is the last permanent village with ancient prayer wheels, stone mani walls, and traditional terraced agriculture.

7. 19-Day Physical Endurance & Conditioning +

Rated Strenuous (4.5/5). Involves significant daily ascents and descents across stone stairways, pass crossings, and rocky glacial moraines. Prior hiking experience is strongly recommended.

8. Flights to Tumlingtar & 4WD Logistics +

We provide flights between Kathmandu and Tumlingtar plus private 4WD Scorpio jeeps to Num and Seduwa, ensuring reliable transport and eliminating long public bus rides.

9. Teahouses and Wilderness Shelters +

Accommodation consists of comfortable family-run lodges in lower villages and rustic stone shelters at Khongma Danda, Dobate, Yangle Kharka, Langmale, and Base Camp with warm dining hearths.

10. High-Altitude Medical Protocols & Oxygen +

Your guide carries pulse oximeters, emergency oxygen, Diamox, and Garmin inReach satellite communication. Helipads are available at Seduwa, Yangle Kharka, and Base Camp.

11. Best Weather Seasons (Spring vs Autumn) +

Spring (March–May) brings blooming rhododendron forests and mild days. Autumn (September–November) offers crystal clear visibility and stable mountain weather for Shipton La.

Essential Clarifications

Frequently Asked Questions

Direct answers regarding Makalu Base Camp, Shipton La Pass, permits, physical fitness, lodges, and flights.

How difficult is the Makalu Base Camp Trek? +

The trek is graded Strenuous (4.5/5). It requires crossing Shipton La Pass (4,216m) twice and trekking up to 4,870m at Base Camp through rugged wilderness terrain. A solid baseline of cardiovascular stamina is essential.

What permits are required for Makalu Base Camp? +

You require the Makalu-Barun National Park Entry Permit and the Trekkers' Information Management System (TIMS) Card. Both permits are 100% arranged and included in your package.

How difficult is crossing Shipton La Pass (4,216m)? +

Shipton La is a non-technical high alpine pass. The trail involves rocky stone steps and moraine walking past Kalo Pokhari lake. Microspikes may be used if early spring snow is present.

What accommodation is available on the Makalu trek? +

You stay in family-run teahouses in Num, Seduwa, and Tashi Gaon. In Khongma Danda, Dobate, Yangle Kharka, Langmale, and Base Camp, accommodation is in rustic stone mountain shelters with warm dining hearths.

Are all domestic flights and 4WD jeeps included? +

Yes! The package includes flights Kathmandu ⇄ Tumlingtar, private 4WD Scorpio jeep transfers between Tumlingtar and Num, and airport transfers in Kathmandu.

How is altitude sickness managed on this trek? +

Our itinerary features a dedicated acclimatization rest day at Khongma Danda (3,500m) before crossing Shipton La. Your guide performs twice-daily pulse oximetry checks and carries emergency oxygen.

Can we see Mt. Everest from the Makalu route? +

Yes! From the high ridge above Makalu Base Camp (5,200m), you have panoramic views of Mt. Everest’s rare Kangshung Face, Lhotse, Chamlang, and Baruntse.

When is the best time to trek Makalu Base Camp? +

Spring (March to May) and Autumn (September to November) are prime. Spring brings blooming rhododendrons and orchids, while Autumn provides the clearest visibility of Makalu's black granite face.

How does the 15% flexible deposit work? +

You pay 15% ($328) online to secure flight seats, national park permits, and your guide team. The remaining 85% is payable upon arrival in Kathmandu. Deposits have lifetime validity.

What gear is provided free by Best Treks Nepal? +

We provide a complimentary 100L waterproof duffel bag, high-grade -20°C expedition down sleeping bag, and branded trek shirt upon your arrival in Kathmandu.

Is emergency helicopter evacuation available in Makalu? +

Yes. Helicopter rescue coordinates can be dispatched directly to Seduwa, Yangle Kharka, and Base Camp in coordination with your emergency travel insurance.

Can solo trekkers book this trek? +

Yes. Unlike restricted areas, Makalu-Barun National Park permits solo trekkers. Solo travelers can book private treks or join one of our guaranteed small-group departures.
