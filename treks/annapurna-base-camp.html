Himalayan Classic Sanctuary

The Annapurna Base Camp Experience

The Annapurna Base Camp (ABC) Trek takes you directly into the inner sanctum of the Annapurna mountain range. Unlike other treks where mountains remain in the distance, stepping into the Annapurna Sanctuary places you at the center of a colossal natural amphitheater, encircled by the towering south face of Annapurna I (8,091m), Annapurna South (7,219m), Hiunchuli (6,441m), and the dramatic fishtail spire of Machhapuchhre (6,993m).

Our comprehensive 11-day route begins with the classic stone stairways through lush Gurung villages and rhododendron forests to Poon Hill (3,210m) for a legendary golden sunrise over both Dhaulagiri and Annapurna ranges. From there, you descend into the Modi Khola river gorge, climbing past bamboo groves and alpine tundra to Machhapuchhre Base Camp (3,700m) and Annapurna Base Camp (4,130m). On your return, soothe tired muscles in the natural thermal hot springs at Jhinu Danda.

Daily Route Blueprint

11-Day Annapurna Base Camp Detailed Itinerary

Ascend step-by-step from Pokhara through Poon Hill, Chhomrong, and the Modi Khola canyon to Annapurna Base Camp (4,130m) and Jhinu Hot Springs.

Day 1 Drive Pokhara to Nayapul (1,070m) & Trek to Tikhedhunga (1,540m)
1,540m 4 hrs / 9 km +

Morning scenic private vehicle drive from Pokhara to Nayapul (1.5 hrs). Cross the Modi Khola suspension bridge and follow the Bhurungdi Khola river past Birethanti and Hile, ascending gently to the pleasant settlement of Tikhedhunga (1,540m).

Sherpa Trail Advice: Rest well tonight. Tomorrow begins with the famous 3,200 stone steps of Ulleri.
Day 2 Trek Tikhedhunga to Ghorepani (2,860m) via Ulleri 3,200 Steps
2,860m 6 hrs / 12 km +

Climb the iconic 3,200 stone staircase up to the picturesque Magar village of Ulleri (2,070m). Enter ancient oak and blooming rhododendron forests, climbing steadily past Banthanti and Nangethanti to reach Ghorepani (2,860m) with sweeping views of Dhaulagiri.

Day 3 Dawn Hike to Poon Hill (3,210m) & Trek to Tadapani (2,630m)
3,210m 6 hrs / 11 km +

Pre-dawn 45-minute climb to the summit of Poon Hill (3,210m) for a world-famous golden sunrise over Dhaulagiri I (8,167m), Annapurna I (8,091m), Annapurna South, Nilgiri, and Machhapuchhre. Return to Ghorepani for breakfast, then trek along the ridge through mossy forests to Tadapani (2,630m).

Day 4 Trek Tadapani to Chhomrong (2,170m) — The Gateway Village
2,170m 5 hrs / 9 km +

Descend through dense rhododendron forest to the Kimrong Khola river, then climb up past terraced farm fields into the grand Gurung village of Chhomrong (2,170m). Chhomrong sits directly under the colossal mass of Annapurna South and Machhapuchhre.

Day 5 Trek Chhomrong to Dovan / Himalaya (2,600m)
2,600m 6 hrs / 11 km +

Descend 2,500 stone stairs to cross the Chhomrong Khola bridge, then ascend through Sinuwa into the deep Modi Khola river canyon. Trek through thick bamboo and oak forests past Bamboo to Dovan (2,600m) or Himalaya Hotel (2,920m).

Day 6 Trek Dovan to Machhapuchhre Base Camp (MBC, 3,700m)
3,700m 5 hrs / 9 km +

Climb past Hinku Cave and Deurali (3,200m) as the valley opens up and trees give way to alpine shrubs and towering rock walls. Enter the gates of the Annapurna Sanctuary at Machhapuchhre Base Camp (3,700m), offering spectacular close-up views of Fishtail, Hiunchuli, and Annapurna III.

Sherpa Trail Advice: MBC is situated in a dramatic alpine basin. Drink hot garlic soup and stay hydrated as we prepare for tomorrow's early sunrise push into ABC.
Day 7 Sunrise Trek to Annapurna Base Camp (4,130m) Amphitheater
4,130m ⭐ ABC Summit Day +

Gentle 2-hour morning ascent alongside the lateral moraine into the heart of the Annapurna Base Camp (4,130m)! Be captivated by an astonishing 360-degree panorama: Annapurna I (8,091m), Annapurna South (7,219m), Hiunchuli (6,441m), Gangapurna (7,455m), and Machhapuchhre (6,993m) rising sheer above you. Spend the day exploring the glacier basin and watching sunset over the south face.

Sherpa Sanctuary Advice: Sunset and sunrise at ABC are once-in-a-lifetime spectacles. Wrap up in your down jacket and watch the pink and gold alpenglow illuminate Annapurna I!
Day 8 Dawn Panorama at ABC & Descend to Bamboo (2,310m)
2,310m 6.5 hrs / 14 km +

Relish the magical sunrise over the Annapurna glacier one last time. After a warm breakfast, retrace your steps down through Machhapuchhre Base Camp, Deurali, and Himalaya Hotel, enjoying the rapid increase in oxygen as you reach Bamboo (2,310m).

Day 9 Trek Bamboo to Jhinu Danda Natural Hot Springs (1,780m)
1,780m 5 hrs / 9 km +

Climb up to Sinuwa and across the Chhomrong stone steps, then take the side trail descending to Jhinu Danda (1,780m). In the afternoon, walk 20 minutes down to the banks of the Modi Khola to soak in the natural thermal hot spring pools.

Sherpa Hot Springs Tip: Bring your swimsuit and a small towel to Jhinu Danda. Soaking in the 38°C hot pools beside the roaring mountain river is pure bliss!
Day 10 Trek Jhinu to Siwai / Nayapul & Private Drive to Pokhara (820m)
820m 3 hrs Trek + 2.5 hrs Drive +

Cross the high suspension bridge at Jhinu Danda and walk along the Modi river valley to Siwai or Nayapul. Board our private vehicle for the scenic drive back to Pokhara (820m). Check into your lakeside hotel and celebrate your ABC sanctuary completion!

Day 11 Scenic Tourist Bus / Flight Pokhara to Kathmandu (1,400m)
1,400m Bus / 25 min Flight +

Travel from Pokhara back to Kathmandu by deluxe tourist bus (or optional 25-minute mountain flight). Farewell dinner in Thamel celebrating your successful expedition.

1. What is the Annapurna Sanctuary Amphitheater? +

The Annapurna Sanctuary is a high glacial basin oval-shaped amphitheater at 4,130m surrounded by an unbroken ring of peaks above 7,000m. Entering through the narrow canyon between Hiunchuli and Machhapuchhre opens up into this sacred natural bowl.

2. Altitude & Acclimatization at ABC (4,130m) +

Because the trek begins low at 1,070m and climbs gradually via Poon Hill (3,210m) and MBC (3,700m), your body acclimatizes smoothly. Sleeping at 4,130m is very safe with our staged pace.

3. Fitness Requirements (Moderate 3/5) +

The route involves significant stone staircases (such as the 3,200 steps of Ulleri and the stairs of Chhomrong). Good cardiovascular fitness and leg endurance are beneficial.

4. Best Photography Hours at ABC +

Golden hour sunrise on the vertical south face of Annapurna I (8,091m) and evening alpenglow on Machhapuchhre provide world-class photography opportunities.

5. Permits Included (ACAP & TIMS) +

Annapurna Conservation Area Permit (ACAP) and TIMS cards are 100% included in our price. We arrange all permits in advance.

6. Travel Insurance Requirements +

Travel insurance covering high-altitude trekking up to 4,500m and emergency helicopter evacuation is required.

7. Teahouses in the Sanctuary +

Teahouses at MBC and ABC are cozy with shared twin rooms and wood-stove heated dining halls. We pre-book all rooms in advance.

8. Temperature & Layering (-8°C to 18°C) +

Lower elevations (Nayapul, Chhomrong) are warm (15°C to 22°C), while nights at ABC drop between 0°C and -8°C. A down jacket and -15°C sleeping bag are provided free.

9. Food & Organic Dining Options +

Full board 3 meals daily are included. Menus feature Dal Bhat, pasta, momo dumplings, potato rosti, pancakes, and organic Himalayan honey.

10. Jhinu Danda Hot Springs Protocol +

The natural thermal pools at Jhinu Danda are situated on the Modi Khola riverbank. Swimwear is required.

11. Medical Safety & Pulse Oximetry +

Our guides carry medical pulse oximeters, first aid kits, and stay in direct contact with our Pokhara Operations desk.

12. Flexible Deposit & Rescheduling +

Book with a 15% deposit ($135). Free date changes up to 30 days prior with lifetime deposit protection.

Frequently Asked Questions

Direct answers from our senior trail team covering Annapurna Base Camp logistics, weather, and preparation.

How difficult is the Annapurna Base Camp Trek (Moderate 3/5)? +

ABC is graded as moderate. The highest sleeping altitude is 4,130m (compared to 5,180m on EBC), making altitude sickness much less frequent. The primary physical challenge is walking up and down stone staircases around Ulleri and Chhomrong.

Why is Poon Hill included in the 11-day itinerary? +

Poon Hill (3,210m) adds a sensational panoramic sunrise over Dhaulagiri and Annapurna massifs while naturally aiding your high-altitude acclimatization before entering the inner sanctuary.

How do we visit the Jhinu Danda hot springs? +

On Day 9, after descending from Bamboo to Jhinu Danda, you can walk 20 minutes down to the Modi riverbank where 3 clean cement thermal pools are situated. Entrance fees are included in Comfort Plus.

What is the difference between ABC and MBC? +

Machhapuchhre Base Camp (MBC, 3,700m) sits at the entry gateway to the sanctuary beneath Fishtail peak. Annapurna Base Camp (ABC, 4,130m) is 2 hours further up, situated right at the base of the massive south face of Annapurna I.

How many hours do we hike per day? +

Average daily trekking time is 5 to 6 hours with plenty of tea stops, photography breaks, and a relaxed 1-hour lunch break in scenic mountain lodges.

Do I need travel insurance for Annapurna Base Camp? +

Yes, travel insurance covering high-altitude trekking up to 4,500m and emergency helicopter rescue is mandatory.

How does the 15% flexible deposit policy work? +

You pay 15% ($135) online to confirm your booking dates. The 85% balance is paid in Nepal upon arrival. Your deposit has lifetime validity with free date changes up to 30 days prior.

What are teahouse facilities like at ABC and MBC? +

Teahouses at MBC and ABC have shared twin rooms, heavy quilts, and wood-stove heated dining rooms. Hot showers and device charging are available throughout the route.

Is it possible to take a helicopter return from ABC? +

Yes! Our Luxury VIP & Heli Return tier includes a direct 15-minute Airbus H125 helicopter flight from Annapurna Base Camp (4,130m) directly to Pokhara airport, avoiding the 3-day descent.

What permits are included in the package? +

Both the Annapurna Conservation Area Project (ACAP) permit and TIMS card are fully included and processed by our office prior to your arrival.

What meals are included during the trek? +

Full board (Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner) is included for all days on trek. You can order fresh items from the lodge menus such as Dal Bhat, momos, noodles, eggs, soups, and hot tea.

What is the best month for the Annapurna Base Camp Trek? +

October–November offers crystal-clear autumn skies and crisp mountain views. March–April offers warmer temperatures and vibrant blooming red rhododendron forests.
