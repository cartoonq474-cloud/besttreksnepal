The Grand Himalayan Round Circuit

The Complete Annapurna Circuit Expedition

The Annapurna Circuit Trek is widely considered one of the greatest long-distance mountain treks on Earth. Spanning dramatic transition zones from sub-tropical green rice paddies and pine forests in the Marshyangdi Valley to the arid, Tibetan-influenced high desert of Manang and Mustang, this 14-day trek offers unmatched ecological and cultural diversity.

The climax of the journey is crossing Thorong La Pass (5,416m) — one of the highest navigable trekking passes in the world. After descending from the snows into the sacred pilgrimage town of Muktinath (3,760m), you follow the ancient trans-Himalayan salt trading route along the Kali Gandaki River, taste organic apple cider in Marpha, soak in natural hot springs at Tatopani, and watch sunrise over Dhaulagiri and Annapurna I from Poon Hill (3,210m).

Thorong La Pass (5,416m)

Stand amidst fluttering prayer flags on the highest pass in Nepal, with views extending into Mustang and Tibet.

Sacred Muktinath Temple

Explore the holy pilgrimage shrine venerated by Hindus and Buddhists with 108 brass water spouts and eternal natural gas flames.

Kali Gandaki River Gorge

Trek through the world's deepest canyon carved between 8,000m giants Annapurna I and Dhaulagiri.

Manang & Ice Lake Acclimatization

Spend 2 full acclimatization days exploring Gurung & Manangi culture, Gangapurna Lake, and Braga Monastery.

Poon Hill Golden Sunrise

Watch 360° dawn light illuminate Dhaulagiri (8,167m), Annapurna South, Nilgiri, and Machhapuchhre.

Tatopani Natural Hot Springs

Relax your sore muscles in geothermal natural mineral hot springs on the banks of the Kali Gandaki River.

Daily Route Blueprint

14-Day Annapurna Circuit Detailed Itinerary

Carefully structured upward acclimatization profile through Manang, high pass crossing over Thorong La (5,416m), and descent into Mustang & Kali Gandaki.

Day 1 Drive Kathmandu to Besisahar & Dharapani (1,860m) by Private 4WD Jeep
1,860m 7 hrs Drive +

Early departure from Kathmandu along the Prithvi Highway beside the Trishuli River to Dumre and Besisahar. Board a private 4WD Scorpio Jeep to navigate the rugged Marshyangdi river gorge, passing waterfalls and terraced rice paddies to reach Dharapani (1,860m).

Sherpa Trail Advice: Using our private 4WD jeep to Dharapani saves 2 dusty days of dirt road walking, letting you start your trek in clean, crisp mountain air.
Day 2 Trek Dharapani to Chame (2,670m)
2,670m 5.5 hrs / 15 km +

Trek through pine and fir forests, entering the Manang district at Danakyu. Ascend past apple orchards and suspension bridges to Timang (2,750m) for breathtaking views of Manaslu (8,163m). Continue through Koto to Chame, the administrative headquarters of Manang.

Day 3 Trek Chame to Upper Pisang (3,300m)
3,300m 5 hrs / 14 km +

Walk through dense pine forests as the valley opens up. Marvel at the curved rock wall of Paungda Danda, rising 1,500m above the riverbed. Arrive at Upper Pisang for dramatic views of Annapurna II and IV across the valley floor.

Day 4 Trek Upper Pisang to Manang (3,540m) via Ghyaru & Ngawal
3,540m 6.5 hrs / 19 km +

Take the high scenic route through Ghyaru (3,670m) and Ngawal (3,660m). This elevated cliffside trail offers unmatched views of the entire Annapurna range. Visit ancient Buddhist chortens before descending into the stone village of Manang.

Sherpa Trail Advice: Taking the high route via Ghyaru today is tougher, but it triggers early acclimatization that makes passing Thorong La much easier.
Day 5 Acclimatization Day in Manang: Hike to Ice Lake (4,600m) or Gangapurna
4,600m 5 hrs / 8 km +

Essential acclimatization day. Take a day hike up to Chongkor Viewpoint / Gangapurna Lake (3,800m) or challenge yourself on the steep trail to Kicho Tal / Ice Lake (4,600m). Attend the afternoon Himalayan Rescue Association (HRA) altitude lecture in Manang.

Day 6 Trek Manang to Yak Kharka (4,050m)
4,050m 4 hrs / 10 km +

Leave Manang village, ascending through alpine pastures with grazing yaks and blue sheep. Cross a wooden bridge over the Jharsing Khola to reach Yak Kharka (4,050m).

Day 7 Trek Yak Kharka to Thorong Phedi (4,450m) / High Camp (4,850m)
4,850m 4.5 hrs / 7 km +

Trek along the river valley, crossing a suspension bridge before a short steep ascent through scree slopes to Thorong Phedi (4,450m). Depending on group fitness and weather, push up to Thorong High Camp (4,850m) for an early night's rest before the pass.

Day 8 Cross Thorong La Pass (5,416m) & Trek down to Muktinath (3,760m)
5,416m 🚩 High Pass Day +

3:30 AM alpine departure. Climb steady snow slopes to the pinnacle summit of Thorong La Pass (5,416m)! Celebrate with high-altitude photos at the world-famous prayer flag signboard. Descend 1,600m into the Mustang district to sacred Muktinath (3,760m).

Sherpa Pass Advice: Put on your windproof outer shell and heavy mittens before leaving High Camp. The early morning wind at 5,000m is cold, but the summit feeling is pure euphoria!
Day 9 Visit Muktinath Temple & Trek to Jomsom & Marpha (2,670m)
2,670m 5.5 hrs / 16 km +

Morning visit to Muktinath Temple to view the 108 sacred water spouts and eternal flame. Descend through the arid Mustang landscape to Kagbeni and trek along the windy Kali Gandaki riverbed to Jomsom and the charming whitewashed village of Marpha, famous for apple orchards and brandy.

Day 10 Drive/Trek Marpha to Tatopani Hot Springs (1,190m)
1,190m 4 hrs Drive / Trek +

Travel through the Kali Gandaki Gorge between Dhaulagiri (8,167m) and Annapurna I (8,091m). Arrive at Tatopani (1,190m) and spend the afternoon soaking in natural geothermal hot mineral springs beside the river.

Day 11 Trek Tatopani to Ghorepani (2,860m)
2,860m 6.5 hrs / 17 km +

Cross the Kali Gandaki River and ascend through Sikha and Chitre villages. Climb through dense rhododendron forests to the mountain ridge village of Ghorepani (2,860m).

Day 12 Poon Hill Sunrise (3,210m), Trek to Birethanti & Drive to Pokhara
3,210m 5 hrs Trek + 1.5 hrs Drive +

5:00 AM hike to Poon Hill (3,210m) for a golden sunrise over Dhaulagiri, Annapurna I, Nilgiri, and Machhapuchhre. Descend through Ulleri stone stairs to Birethanti and drive by private vehicle to lakeside Pokhara (820m).

Day 13 Drive Pokhara to Kathmandu by Tourist Bus / Optional Flight
1,400m 6.5 hrs Bus +

Scenic morning drive from Pokhara back to Kathmandu along the Marshyangdi and Trishuli rivers (or optional 25-minute flight upgrade). Check into hotel and enjoy free evening in Thamel.

Day 14 Kathmandu Celebration Breakfast & Departure Transfer
1,400m Airport Transfer +

Celebratory breakfast in Kathmandu. Private transfer to Tribhuvan International Airport for your international departure flight.

1. Thorong La Pass Crossing Protocol (5,416m) +

Day 8 is the pass day. Departure from High Camp (4,850m) is scheduled for 3:30 AM to beat afternoon ridge winds. The climb takes 3 to 4 hours to reach Thorong La Pass (5,416m), followed by a 3 to 4-hour steep descent to Muktinath (3,760m).

2. High-Altitude Acclimatization Strategy +

The itinerary incorporates 2 full nights in Manang (3,540m) with an active acclimatization day hiking to Ice Lake (4,600m) or Gangapurna viewpoint. This guarantees a 98.6% pass success rate.

3. Physical Fitness Requirements (Grade 3.5/5) +

You should be comfortable walking 5 to 7 hours per day with a 5kg daypack. 6–8 weeks of cardio training, stair climbing, and weekend hiking is recommended prior to arrival.

4. Road Construction & Bypassing Strategy +

We use private 4WD Jeeps to skip dusty lower road construction, placing you directly on clean mountain trails like the NATT (New Annapurna Trekking Trails) network from Dharapani and Upper Pisang.

5. ACAP & TIMS Permit Regulations +

All trekkers require an Annapurna Conservation Area Permit (ACAP) and TIMS card. We process both in advance so there are zero delays at checkpoints.

6. Travel Insurance for 5,500m & Emergency Rescue +

Insurance covering high-altitude trekking up to 5,500m and helicopter evacuation is mandatory. Recommended providers include World Nomads (Explorer), Global Rescue, and Allianz.

7. Teahouses & Lodging Facilities +

Lodges along the Annapurna Circuit are among the best in Nepal. Standard rooms feature twin beds; attached bathrooms are available in lower villages (Pokhara, Besisahar, Jomsom, Tatopani).

8. Temperature Management & Gear Needs (-15°C) +

Thorong Phedi and High Camp temperatures drop to -10°C to -15°C. We provide a complimentary -15°C down sleeping bag and 100L duffel bag.

9. Food, Meals & Drinking Water Safety +

Full board (3 meals daily) is included. Enjoy Dal Bhat, Thakali sets, Marpha apple pies, soups, and hot drinks. Purified drinking water is available throughout the route.

10. Wi-Fi, Phone Connectivity & Solar Power +

Ncell and NTC 4G mobile coverage is good in most villages up to Manang and Muktinath. Teahouses offer solar charging and Wi-Fi for a small fee.

11. Diamox & Medical Safety Protocols +

Guides carry medical first-aid kits, pulse oximeters, and bottled oxygen. Diamox can be taken under guide guidance to speed acclimatization.

12. Flexible Deposit & Rescheduling Terms +

Book with a 15% deposit ($178). Free date rescheduling up to 30 days before departure with lifetime deposit protection.

Essential Clarifications

Frequently Asked Questions

Direct answers from our senior expedition team covering Thorong La Pass logistics, road conditions, and safety.

How difficult is crossing Thorong La Pass (5,416m)? +

Thorong La Pass is a non-technical high-altitude pass. While it requires 6 to 8 hours of steady hiking on steep snow or rock trails starting at 3:30 AM, no ropes or climbing gear are needed. Proper acclimatization in Manang makes it achievable for any fit hiker.

Has road construction ruined the Annapurna Circuit? +

No. We use private 4WD Jeeps to bypass dusty road sections in the lower valley and switch to the NATT (New Annapurna Trekking Trails) network, taking scenic footpaths via Ghyaru, Ngawal, and mountain ridges away from motor traffic.

What time do we start on the day of Thorong La Pass? +

Departure from High Camp (4,850m) is usually set for 3:30 AM to 4:00 AM. This ensures you reach the 5,416m summit pass by 7:30 AM before high winds develop after 10:00 AM.

What permits are required for the Annapurna Circuit? +

You need an Annapurna Conservation Area Permit (ACAP) and a TIMS card. Both are fully included in our package and arranged prior to trek departure.

What are the teahouse accommodations like on the circuit? +

Teahouses along the Annapurna Circuit are very comfortable with clean twin-bed rooms, heated dining halls, and varied menus. Private attached bathrooms are available in lower towns.

How do you handle Acute Mountain Sickness (AMS)? +

Our guides carry pulse oximeters to test blood oxygen saturation twice daily. If mild AMS symptoms occur, we rest or use Diamox. If severe symptoms arise, our 24/7 satellite link coordinates immediate helicopter evacuation.

How does the 15% deposit and cancellation policy work? +

You pay 15% ($178) to lock in your dates. The remaining balance is paid in Kathmandu upon arrival. Deposits have lifetime validity with free date rescheduling up to 30 days prior.

What meals are included during the 14 days? +

Full board (Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner) is included for all 14 days. You can select dishes from lodge menus including Dal Bhat, Thakali sets, pasta, soups, and hot tea.

Can we take a flight from Jomsom to Pokhara to save days? +

Yes! Our Luxury VIP tier includes a 20-minute mountain flight from Jomsom to Pokhara, bypassing the drive through Tatopani for travelers short on time.

What travel insurance policy do I need? +

Travel insurance covering high-altitude trekking up to 5,500m and emergency helicopter evacuation is required. Recommended providers include World Nomads, Global Rescue, and Allianz.

Is microspikes or crampons necessary for Thorong La? +

During spring and autumn, microspikes are recommended for early morning packed snow on the final 300m approach to the pass. Your guide will advise you on trail conditions.

Can we add Tilicho Lake (4,919m) to this itinerary? +

Yes! We frequently add 3 extra days to include Tilicho Lake (the world's highest glacial lake). Contact us on WhatsApp for custom Tilicho itineraries.
