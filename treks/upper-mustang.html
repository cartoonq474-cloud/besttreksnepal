The Forbidden Kingdom of Lo

The Upper Mustang Walled Kingdom Trek

The Upper Mustang Walled Kingdom Trek (14 Days) is a rare pilgrimage into one of the most culturally preserved, geographically dramatic corners of the Tibetan plateau. Known as the Kingdom of Lo, this semi-independent monarchy remained strictly closed to outsiders until 1992. Situated north of the main Himalayan spine in the rain shadow of Dhaulagiri and Annapurna, Upper Mustang boasts a moon-like desert landscape of eroded sandstone pillars, deep river canyons, ochre cliffs, and fortified white-washed stone villages.

The crown jewel of the expedition is the 15th-century walled capital of Lo Manthang (3,840m), founded by the warrior king Ame Pal. Here, ancient Tibetan Buddhist monasteries such as Jampa Gompa, Thubchen Gompa, and Chode Gompa safeguard 600-year-old silk thangkas and gold-inscribed murals. Venture into the multi-story cliff dwellings of Chhoser (Jhong Sky Caves), marvel at the blood-red cliffs of Dhakmar, and receive blessings at Ghar Gompa, one of the oldest surviving Nyingma monasteries in the world.

15th-Century Walled Capital of Lo Manthang

Walk inside the ancient fortified city walls to explore the 4-story King's Palace and 600-year-old Gompas.

2,000-Year-Old Chhoser Sky Caves

Climb into the multi-story honeycomb cliff caves of Jhong used for burial, refuge, and monastic meditation.

Red Sandstone Canyons & Dhakmar

Marvel at surreal blood-red geological amphitheaters and wind-sculpted fluted rock formations.

8th-Century Ghar Gompa (Gyakar)

Visit one of the oldest Nyingma monasteries founded by Guru Padmasambhava with carved stone deities.

Rain Shadow Trekking (Summer/Monsoon Ideal)

Experience clear, dry, and rain-free trekking even during Nepal's summer monsoon months (June–August).

Living Tibetan Culture of the Loba

14-Day Kingdom Blueprint

14-Day Upper Mustang Detailed Itinerary

Fly to Jomsom, cross into the restricted Kingdom of Lo at Kagbeni, spend two full days in the walled capital of Lo Manthang, and return via Ghar Gompa and Dhakmar.

Day 1 Scenic Flight Kathmandu to Pokhara (820m) & Lakeside Leisure
820m 25 min Flight +

Take a 25-minute morning flight from Kathmandu to the lakeside city of Pokhara (820m) with sweeping views of the Annapurna range. Check into your hotel, enjoy a briefing with your Sherpa guide, and spend the afternoon relaxing by Phewa Lake.

Day 2 Mountain Flight Pokhara to Jomsom (2,720m) & Trek to Kagbeni (2,810m)
2,810m 20m Flight + 3 hrs Trek +

Early morning flight from Pokhara to Jomsom (2,720m) through the world's deepest gorge between Annapurna and Dhaulagiri. Trek along the wide, windy Kali Gandaki riverbed to the medieval gateway oasis of Kagbeni (2,810m) with its 500-year-old red Gompa.

Day 3 Enter Restricted Upper Mustang: Trek Kagbeni to Chele (3,050m)
3,050m 5.5 hrs / 15 km +

Present your special Restricted Area Permit at the Kagbeni police checkpoint and cross the official border into Upper Mustang. Follow the riverbank past the white-washed village of Tangbe, red chortens, and apple orchards to cross the Kali Gandaki bridge into Chele (3,050m).

Day 4 Trek Chele to Syangboche (3,800m) via Taklam La & Chungsi Cave
3,800m 6 hrs / 14 km +

Climb steeply over the Taklam La Pass (3,624m) and Dajori La (3,735m) with expansive views of Tilicho Peak and Damodar Himal. Visit the sacred self-arisen meditation cave of Guru Padmasambhava at Chungsi Cave before reaching Syangboche (3,800m).

Day 5 Trek Syangboche to Ghami (3,520m) via Nyi La Pass (4,010m)
3,520m 5.5 hrs / 12 km +

Cross the Yamada La (3,850m) and climb to the highest pass on the classic trail: Nyi La Pass (4,010m), the historic boundary of the Lo Kingdom. Descend to the large, green village of Ghami (3,520m) surrounded by dramatic red cliffs.

Day 6 Trek Ghami to Tsarang / Charang (3,560m) past Longest Mani Wall
3,560m 4.5 hrs / 11 km +

Pass the longest sacred Mani Wall in Nepal (over 300m long) carved with Tibetan prayers. Cross the Choya La Pass (3,870m) and arrive in Tsarang (3,560m), the second-largest town in Mustang featuring a massive 5-story royal palace and 14th-century red monastery.

Day 7 Trek Tsarang to the Walled Capital of Lo Manthang (3,840m)
3,840m ⭐ Walled Kingdom +

Descend the Tsarang canyon and cross the Lo La Pass (3,950m). From the pass crest, catch your first breathtaking view of the white-washed, fortified walled city of Lo Manthang (3,840m) rising out of the desert plain. Enter through the single historic city gate and check into your heritage lodge.

Sherpa Cultural Highlight: Lo Manthang was built in 1440 by King Ame Pal. The city's 6-meter-tall walls have protected ancient Buddhist art for over 580 years!
Day 8 Exploration: Chhoser 2,000-Year-Old Sky Caves & Ancient Gompas
3,900m ⭐ Sky Caves Wonder +

Take a day trip north toward the Tibetan border to Chhoser. Climb inside the multi-story Jhong Sky Cave carved into vertical sandstone cliffs over 2,000 years ago. Visit the red monastery of Nyphu Gompa built into the rock face. In the afternoon, return to Lo Manthang to tour Jampa Gompa (God House of Maitreya) and Thubchen Gompa.

Day 9 Trek Lo Manthang to Dhakmar (3,820m) via 8th-Century Ghar Gompa
3,820m 6.5 hrs / 16 km +

Take the western return route crossing the Marang La Pass (4,230m). Visit ancient Ghar Gompa (Gyakar), an 8th-century sanctuary older than Samye Monastery in Tibet, renowned for its wish-fulfilling stone paintings. Continue down to the dramatic red cliffs of Dhakmar (3,820m).

Day 10 Trek Dhakmar to Ghiling (3,570m) through Sculpted Red Canyons
3,570m 5.5 hrs / 15 km +

Follow the trail through wind-sculpted golden canyon walls past Ghami and Jaite to reach the peaceful settlement of Ghiling (3,570m) with its beautiful mud-brick chortens.

Day 11 Trek Ghiling to Chhusang (2,980m)
2,980m 5 hrs / 14 km +

Descend past Syangboche and Chele, soaking in vistas of Nilgiri and Annapurna I across the southern horizon. Reach the riverbank village of Chhusang (2,980m).

Day 12 Trek Chhusang to Jomsom (2,720m) via Kagbeni
2,720m 6 hrs / 18 km +

Trek past Tangbe to Kagbeni, completing your exit check at the immigration post. Follow the Kali Gandaki riverbed into Jomsom (2,720m) to celebrate your successful expedition completion.

Day 13 Scenic Flights Jomsom to Pokhara & Pokhara to Kathmandu (1,400m)
1,400m 2 Flights +

Morning mountain flight from Jomsom to Pokhara, followed by a connecting flight back to Kathmandu. Check into your hotel and enjoy free time for souvenir shopping in Thamel.

Day 14 Final Departure / Grand Farewell Celebration Dinner in Kathmandu
1,400m Celebration +

Join the Best Treks Nepal team for a celebratory farewell dinner, receive your official trek certificate, and transfer to Tribhuvan International Airport for your flight home.

1. Upper Mustang $500 RAP Permit Rules +

The Nepal Department of Immigration charges $500 USD per person for the 10-day Upper Mustang Restricted Area Permit (RAP), plus $50/day for additional days. A minimum of 2 trekkers and a licensed guide are mandatory by law. This fee is 100% included in our price.

2. Why Trek in Monsoon / Summer (June–August)? +

Upper Mustang is located in the rain shadow of the Annapurna and Dhaulagiri mountain ranges. While the rest of Nepal experiences monsoon rain, Mustang remains completely dry, sunny, and cloudless, making it Nepal's finest summer trek!

3. History of Walled City of Lo Manthang (1440 AD) +

Founded by Ame Pal in 1440, Lo Manthang is a walled fortress with a single entry gate. It served as an independent Tibetan kingdom until Nepal annexed it in the late 18th century, maintaining its own royal raja until 2008.

4. Exploration of 2,000-Year-Old Chhoser Sky Caves +

Over 10,000 man-made caves are carved into Mustang's vertical sandstone cliffs. Jhong Cave in Chhoser features 5 stories and dozens of interconnected rooms used as ancient burial chambers and fortress retreats during tribal warfare.

5. Sacred Significance of Ghar Gompa +

Built in the 8th century by Guru Rinpoche (Padmasambhava) before he built Samye Monastery in Tibet, Ghar Gompa is famous for its intricate painted stone carvings depicting Buddhist deities.

6. Mountain Flights Pokhara ⇄ Jomsom +

You fly between Pokhara and Jomsom (20 minutes each way) through the Kali Gandaki gorge. Flights operate early in the morning before the valley afternoon winds set in.

7. Physical Difficulty & Altitude (3/5) +

Upper Mustang is classified as moderate. The maximum altitude is 3,840m at Lo Manthang (and 4,010m on Nyi La Pass). Gradients are gentle, making this trek suitable for all active walkers.

8. Heritage Lodges & Teahouses +

Lodges in Upper Mustang feature unique Tibetan architecture with flat mud roofs, timber interiors, courtyard hearths, and private twin rooms with warm blankets.

9. High-Desert Wind & Sun Protection +

The Kali Gandaki valley generates strong thermal afternoon winds. A windproof jacket, neck gaiter (buff), UV400 sunglasses, and SPF 50+ sunscreen are indispensable.

10. Temperature Range (-4°C to 26°C) +

Daytime walking temperatures are pleasantly warm (18°C to 24°C in summer and autumn), cooling down to 0°C to -4°C at night in Lo Manthang.

11. Medical Safety & Evacuation +

Your guide carries pulse oximeters and medical kits. Helicopter rescue standbys are easily accessible at Jomsom, Tsarang, and Lo Manthang.

12. Flexible 15% Deposit Guarantee +

Book with a 15% deposit ($283). Free date changes up to 30 days prior with lifetime validity protection.

Frequently Asked Questions

Direct answers from our senior expedition leaders covering Upper Mustang permits, Lo Manthang culture, and weather.

Is the $500 USD Upper Mustang RAP permit included in the package price? +

Yes, 100%! The official $500 USD government Restricted Area Permit (covering 10 days in Upper Mustang), plus the Annapurna Conservation Area Permit (ACAP) and TIMS card are fully included in our package price.

Can solo travelers join this trek? +

Yes. Government regulations require a minimum of 2 trekkers to issue Upper Mustang RAP permits. We pair solo travelers with our confirmed departure groups to fulfill the government requirement seamlessly.

Why is Upper Mustang called the best monsoon / summer trek in Nepal? +

Upper Mustang is geologically situated on the Tibetan Plateau, in the rain shadow of Annapurna and Dhaulagiri. The 8,000m peaks block all monsoon clouds, meaning Upper Mustang receives less than 200mm of rain per year. June, July, and August are dry, sunny, and warm.

What are the Chhoser Sky Caves and how do we visit them? +

The Chhoser caves are over 2,000-year-old multi-story cave dwellings carved into vertical rock faces. During our exploration day from Lo Manthang, we hike or take a short ride north to enter Jhong Cave and explore its interconnected rooms, windows, and ladders.

What is the Tiji Festival in Lo Manthang? +

Tiji (Tenchi) is a 3-day sacred festival held every May in the courtyard of the King's Palace in Lo Manthang. Monks perform sacred masked dances celebrating the victory of the deity Dorje Jono over evil demons. We offer special fixed departure dates for Tiji.

What is Ghar Gompa and why is it famous? +

Ghar Gompa in Gyakar was built in the 8th century by Guru Padmasambhava. According to Tibetan Buddhist tradition, anyone who makes a sincere spiritual wish inside Ghar Gompa will have it granted.

How difficult is the Upper Mustang trek? +

It is graded moderate (3/5). Maximum altitudes reach 3,840m at Lo Manthang (4,010m on Nyi La Pass). The trails feature gentle ascents and descents without extreme passes, making it very accessible for all fit hikers.

What happens if Jomsom flights are delayed due to weather? +

If morning flights between Pokhara and Jomsom are affected by weather, we have backup private 4WD Scorpio jeeps on standby to transfer you safely along the Kali Gandaki highway.

How does the 15% flexible deposit work? +

You pay 15% ($283) online to secure your restricted permit reservation and flights. The remaining 85% is paid upon arrival in Kathmandu. Deposits have lifetime validity with free date changes up to 30 days prior.

What meals and water are provided on the trek? +

Full board (Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner) is included for all 14 days on trek. Menus offer Dal Bhat, Tibetan bread, momos, noodle soups, porridge, eggs, potato stews, and hot tea.

How windy does it get in Upper Mustang? +

The Kali Gandaki gorge creates thermal winds from 11:00 AM to 4:00 PM daily. We start trekking early in the morning (around 7:30 AM) to finish daily walking before the peak afternoon breeze.

What equipment is provided free by Best Treks Nepal? +

We provide a complimentary 100L waterproof duffel bag, a high-grade -15°C down sleeping bag, and a branded trekking shirt upon your arrival in Kathmandu.
