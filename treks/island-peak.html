Combining the classic trek to Everest Base Camp (5,364m) and the sunrise ascent of Kala Patthar (5,545m), this comprehensive 18-day itinerary builds supreme physiological acclimatization before you tackle the glaciated headwall and summit ridge of Island Peak (6,189m / 20,305ft).

Led by certified IFMGA / NMA Sherpa climbing guides with a dedicated 1:2 guide-to-client climbing ratio, you receive full glacier training in Chhukung, fixed-line climbing support with jumars and figure-of-eight descenders, high-camp tented infrastructure with an expedition chef, and an official Nepal Mountaineering Association (NMA) Summit Certificate upon conquest.

18-Day Expedition Blueprint

18-Day Island Peak & EBC Detailed Itinerary

Complete route from Lukla through Namche Bazaar, Tengboche, Dingboche, Everest Base Camp (5,364m), Kala Patthar (5,545m), and the summit of Island Peak (6,189m).

Day 1 Flight Kathmandu / Ramechhap to Lukla (2,846m) & Trek to Phakding (2,610m)
2,610m 35m Flight + 3.5 hrs / 8 km +

Take an early morning mountain flight to Tenzing-Hillary Airport in Lukla (2,846m). Meet your Sherpa climbing guide and porter team, organize gear, and trek along the Dudh Koshi river valley through pine forests and mani walls to Phakding (2,610m).

Day 2 Trek Phakding to Namche Bazaar (3,440m) via Hillary Suspension Bridge
3,440m 6 hrs / 11 km +

Cross suspension bridges decorated with prayer flags, enter Sagarmatha National Park at Monjo, and cross the iconic Hillary Suspension Bridge over the confluence of the Dudh Koshi and Bhote Koshi. Ascend the steep pine ridge to Namche Bazaar (3,440m), the bustling Sherpa capital.

Day 3 Acclimatization Day in Namche Bazaar: Hike to Hotel Everest View (3,880m)
3,880m Viewpoint Acclimatization Day +

Essential acclimatization hike up to Syangboche and Hotel Everest View (3,880m) for panoramic views of Mt. Everest, Lhotse, Ama Dablam, and Thamserku. Visit the Hillary School and Khumjung Gompa.

Day 4 Trek Namche Bazaar to Tengboche Monastery (3,867m)
3,867m 5 hrs / 9.5 km +

Walk along the scenic balcony trail with views of Ama Dablam, descend to Phunki Thenga for lunch, and climb through rhododendron and birch forests to Tengboche Monastery (3,867m). Attend evening monks' prayer ceremonies.

Day 5 Trek Tengboche to Dingboche (4,410m) beneath Ama Dablam
4,410m 5.5 hrs / 11 km +

Descend to Deboche and pass the stone mani walls of Pangboche with close-up views of Ama Dablam. Enter the high alpine Imja Valley and reach the stone-walled summer settlement of Dingboche (4,410m).

Day 6 Acclimatization Hike to Nangkartshang Peak (5,083m)
5,083m Summit Acclimatization Day +

Climb the steep ridge north of Dingboche to Nangkartshang Peak (5,083m) for views of Makalu (8,485m), Lhotse, Ama Dablam, and Island Peak rising above the Imja Glacier.

Day 7 Trek Dingboche to Lobuche (4,940m) via Thokla Pass Memorials
4,940m 5 hrs / 8 km +

Trek along the wide alpine valley to Dughla, climb steeply up Thokla Pass (4,830m) past the stone chortens and memorials honoring fallen Everest mountaineers, and continue alongside the Khumbu Glacier moraine to Lobuche (4,940m).

Day 8 🏔️ Lobuche to Gorak Shep (5,164m) & Trek to Everest Base Camp (5,364m)
5,364m EBC 7.5 hrs / 13 km +

Trek across glacial moraine to Gorak Shep (5,164m), check into lodge, and continue onto the lateral moraine of the Khumbu Glacier to reach Everest Base Camp (5,364m). Stand beneath the seracs and blue ice towers of the Khumbu Icefall before returning to Gorak Shep.

Day 9 🌅 Sunrise Climb of Kala Patthar (5,545m) & Trek to Chhukung (4,730m)
5,545m Kala Patthar 7 hrs / 14 km +

Pre-dawn climb up Kala Patthar (5,545m) for the sunrise panorama of Mt. Everest, Nuptse, and Pumori. Descend to Gorak Shep for breakfast and trek down the valley to Dingboche, continuing east into the Imja Valley to Chhukung (4,730m).

Day 10 Trek Chhukung to Island Peak Base Camp (5,200m) & Pre-Climb Glacier Training
5,200m Base Camp 4 hrs / 6 km +

Trek along the south flank of the moraine past Imja Tsho glacial lake to Island Peak Base Camp (5,200m / Pareshaya Gyab). After lunch in the dining tent, your IFMGA Sherpa climbing guide conducts comprehensive training on fixed rope ascending (jumar), figure-of-eight rappelling, crampon footwork, and ice axe self-arrest.

Day 11 Ascend to High Camp (5,600m) / Glacier Briefing & Early Rest
5,600m High Camp 3 hrs / 3 km +

Climb up a steep rocky gully to Island Peak High Camp (5,600m). Set up high-altitude tents, review summit strategies and rope teams, hydrate, and get early sleep for midnight summit push.

Day 12 ⚡ Summit Push: High Camp to Island Peak Summit (6,189m) & Descend to Chhukung
6,189m SUMMIT 10–12 hrs Summit Day +

Begin summit push at 1:00 AM. Scramble up rocky ridges to Crampon Point (5,800m), rope up for glacier crossing, negotiate crevasses with aluminum ladders if needed, and tackle the famous 45-degree ice headwall on fixed lines to top out on the knife-edge summit of Island Peak (6,189m / 20,305ft)! Enjoy 360-degree views of Lhotse (8,516m), Makalu (8,485m), Ama Dablam (6,812m), and Baruntse. Rappel down fixed lines, pack high camp, and descend to Chhukung (4,730m).

Day 13 Contingency / Weather Reserve Summit Day in Chhukung
4,730m Contingency Day +

Dedicated summit contingency day in case of high winds or snowfall on Day 12. If summit was achieved on schedule, enjoy a well-deserved rest day in Chhukung or leisurely trek to Pangboche.

Day 14 Trek Chhukung to Pangboche / Tengboche (3,867m)
3,867m 5.5 hrs / 12 km +

Descend through the Imja Valley past Dingboche and Pangboche, crossing the Imja Khola to return to Tengboche (3,867m).

Day 15 Trek Tengboche to Namche Bazaar (3,440m)
3,440m 5 hrs / 10 km +

Descend to Phunki Thenga, climb the stone steps past Sanasa and Kyangjuma, and contour around the mountain to Namche Bazaar (3,440m). Celebrate your summit with hot showers, bakeries, and cafes.

Day 16 Trek Namche Bazaar to Lukla (2,846m)
2,846m 7 hrs / 19 km +

Descend the Hillary Suspension Bridge, pass Monjo and Phakding, and make the final gentle climb to Lukla (2,846m). Evening celebration and tips ceremony with your Sherpa climbing guides and porters.

Day 17 Flight Lukla to Kathmandu / Ramechhap & Hotel Transfer
1,400m Flight to KTM +

Morning flight from Lukla to Kathmandu / Ramechhap with private vehicle transfer to your hotel. Rest, relax, and explore Thamel.

Day 18 Celebration Banquet & Presentation of Official NMA Summit Certificates
1,400m Celebration +

Grand celebration dinner in Kathmandu with our mountaineering team and presentation of your official Nepal Mountaineering Association (NMA) Island Peak Summit Certificate.

Mountaineering Manual

Read Before You Climb — Island Peak Knowledge Base

Essential preparation on Island Peak (6,189m), technical climbing gear, jumar ascending, EBC acclimatization, and safety.

1. Island Peak (Imja Tse 6,189m) Mountain Profile +

Named 'Island Peak' in 1953 by Eric Shipton's team because it resembles an island in a sea of ice when viewed from Dingboche. It rises in the Imja Valley directly beneath the sheer South Face of Lhotse (8,516m).

2. The 100m Ice Headwall & Summit Ridge +

The crux of the climb is the 100-meter ice/snow headwall pitched at 45 to 50 degrees, followed by a classic knife-edge exposed summit ridge leading to the 6,189m top. Fixed ropes and ascenders (jumars) are used.

3. Pre-Climb Glacier School in Chhukung +

On Day 10, our IFMGA Sherpa climbing guides conduct an extensive training clinic on fixed line ascending with jumars, figure-of-eight rappelling, crampon footwork on ice, and self-arrest with ice axes.

4. Why EBC + Kala Patthar First? (98.4% Success) +

Trekking to Everest Base Camp (5,364m) and climbing Kala Patthar (5,545m) on Days 8–9 builds red blood cell counts and deep acclimatization, drastically elevating Island Peak summit success from ~65% to 98.4%.

5. 1:2 Sherpa Guide to Client Climbing Ratio +

On summit night, we maintain a strict 1:2 guide ratio. Every pair of climbers is attached to an experienced Sherpa guide who manages fixed-line carabiner transitions, pacing, and crevasse safety.

6. Base Camp (5,200m) & High Camp (5,600m) Infrastructure +

We establish our private base camp at 5,200m with North Face mountain tents, a dedicated kitchen crew providing fresh hot carbohydrates, and advance High Camp at 5,600m to shorten summit morning by 2.5 hours.

7. Technical Climbing Gear Checklist +

Personal gear needed: Double mountaineering boots (e.g. La Sportiva Nepal / G2), C2/C3 crampons, climbing harness, climbing helmet, jumar ascender, figure-8 descender, 2 locking carabiners, and ice axe. (Available for rent in Chhukung/Kathmandu).

8. NMA Climbing Permits & Garbage Deposit +

Island Peak is regulated by the Nepal Mountaineering Association (NMA). All climbing royalties ($250/pax), garbage deposits, and Sherpa insurance are 100% handled and covered in your package.

9. Summit Night Timeline (1:00 AM Departure) +

Wake up at 12:30 AM for hot porridge and tea. Depart High Camp at 1:00 AM with headlamps. Reach Crampon Point at dawn (5:30 AM), summit between 7:30 AM and 9:00 AM, and return to Chhukung by early afternoon.

10. High-Altitude Medical Protocols & Oxygen +

Our team carries medical-grade bottled oxygen, Gamow bag access, pulse oximeters, and a Garmin inReach satellite SOS communicator. Direct emergency helicopter dispatch is available at Chhukung and Dingboche.

11. Best Weather Seasons (Spring vs Autumn) +

Spring (April–May) features warmer temperatures, blooming Khumbu valleys, and stable morning conditions. Autumn (October–November) offers crystal-clear skies, firmer ice conditions on the headwall, and spectacular visibility.

Do I need prior mountaineering experience for Island Peak? +

No prior technical mountaineering experience is required, but excellent physical cardiovascular fitness and trekking experience are essential. Our guides provide full pre-climb training in Chhukung on jumar ascending, crampon footwork, and rappelling.

What is the technical difficulty of the summit headwall? +

The headwall is approximately 100 vertical meters of snow/ice pitched at 45 to 50 degrees. It is protected by fixed ropes. You ascend using a jumar (ascender) with safety carabiners and rappel down using a figure-8 descender.

What permits are included in this expedition package? +

The package includes the official NMA Island Peak Climbing Permit ($250 USD value), NMA garbage deposit, Sagarmatha National Park entry permit, and Khumbu Pasang Lhamu rural municipality permit.

Can I rent technical climbing gear in Nepal? +

Yes! High-quality double mountaineering boots, crampons, harness, helmet, jumar, and ice axe can be rented in Chhukung or Kathmandu for approximately $80–$120 USD for the entire set.

What is the climbing guide-to-client ratio on summit night? +

We maintain a strict 1:2 guide-to-client ratio on summit night (1 certified Sherpa climbing guide per 2 climbers) to ensure maximum safety, personalized pacing, and fixed rope management.

Why is the Everest Base Camp trek included first? +

Trekking to EBC (5,364m) and climbing Kala Patthar (5,545m) on Days 8–9 provides world-class acclimatization. By the time you reach Island Peak, your body is fully adapted to high altitudes, yielding a 98.4% summit rate.

What is the accommodation like at Base Camp & High Camp? +

You sleep in 4-season mountain tents with thick foam mattresses. Base camp features a dining tent with hot meals prepared by our expedition cook. High camp provides advance shelter to shorten summit morning.

Are all domestic Lukla flights included? +

Yes! Round-trip domestic flights between Kathmandu / Ramechhap and Lukla with 15kg luggage allowance (including duffel) are 100% included in all package tiers.

What happens if weather is bad on summit day? +

Our 18-day itinerary includes Day 13 as a dedicated weather contingency reserve day in Chhukung, giving your team a second attempt if high winds or snowfall occur on Day 12.

Do I receive an official climbing certificate? +

Yes! Upon reaching the summit, your guide submits photo verification to the Nepal Mountaineering Association, and you receive an official government-recognized NMA Summit Certificate.

Is emergency helicopter evacuation available? +

Yes. Direct helicopter rescue dispatch is available at Chhukung, Dingboche, and Gorak Shep through our 24/7 Kathmandu operations desk in coordination with your emergency travel insurance.

How does the 15% flexible deposit work? +
