TRANSPARENT VALUE

What Is Included in Our All-Inclusive Packages?

No hidden fees, no surprise extras on the trail. Here is exactly what every Best Treks Nepal package guarantees.

Guaranteed Inclusions

100% COVERED
  • Airport Transfers: Private arrival & departure pick-up at Tribhuvan International Airport (Kathmandu).
  • All Domestic Flights / Transfers: Kathmandu–Lukla, Pokhara flights, or private 4WD jeeps as per itinerary.
  • Teahouse & Hotel Lodging: 3-star Kathmandu/Pokhara hotels + best available mountain lodge twin-share rooms.
  • 3 Full Daily Meals on Trail: Breakfast, lunch, and dinner cooked fresh daily + tea/coffee breaks.
  • Licensed Government Sherpa Guide & Porters: Certified Wilderness First Aid lead guide + 1 porter for every 2 trekkers.
  • All Required Permits & Taxes: TIMS card, National Park entries, Municipal fees, and RAP restricted permits.
  • Safety Gear & Satellite Comms: Pulse oximeter daily checks, medical first-aid kit, down jacket & sleeping bag loaner.
  • Traditional Farewell Dinner: Authentic Nepali cultural performance dinner & official certificate of completion.

Exclusions & Extras

PERSONAL SPENDING
  • International Flights: Airfare from your home country to Kathmandu (KTM) and return flights.
  • Nepal Tourist Visa Fee: Payable upon arrival at Kathmandu airport (\$30 for 15 days / \$50 for 30 days).
  • Travel & Medical Evacuation Insurance: Must cover high-altitude emergency helicopter evacuation up to 6,000m.
  • Personal Trail Expenses: Bottled water/soft drinks, hot showers in high teahouses, battery charging, WiFi cards.
  • Guide & Porter Gratuities: Tipping is traditional in Nepal (approx \$10–\$15/day split per trekker).
  • Personal Trekking Gear: Trekking boots, poles, thermal base layers, and daypack (loaner sleeping bags available).
