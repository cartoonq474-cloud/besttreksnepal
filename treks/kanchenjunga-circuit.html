The Complete Double Traverse

Grand Kanchenjunga Double Circuit (24 Days)

The Grand Kanchenjunga Double Circuit (24 Days) is widely considered the crowning jewel of Himalayan wilderness expeditions. By traversing from the northern river valleys to the southern glacier sanctuaries, this ultimate 24-day itinerary connects Pangpema North Base Camp (5,143m) directly beneath the 3,000-meter north face of Mt. Kanchenjunga (8,586m) with Oktang South Base Camp (4,730m) in the Yalung Valley.

The connecting link between these two grand mountain basins is the remote, high-alpine traverse crossing Sele La Pass (4,480m), Sinion La (4,440m), and Mirgin La (4,480m). Along the journey, you will explore ancient Tibetan monasteries in Ghunsa (3,595m), gaze at the sheer vertical cathedral wall of Mt. Jannu / Kumbhakarna (7,710m), and experience multi-ethnic culture in Yamphudin (1,690m), with all domestic flights and private 4WD jeeps included.

Dual Base Camps: Pangpema & Oktang

Stand at both North (5,143m) and South (4,730m) Base Camps facing the contrasting faces of Mt. Kanchenjunga.

Sele La & Mirgin La High Passes (4,480m)

Cross the rugged alpine watershed separating the Ghunsa and Simbua river valleys with views of Makalu.

Mt. Jannu / Kumbhakarna (7,710m)

Marvel at one of the world's most formidable alpine cathedral walls rising vertically from Kambachen.

Ghunsa & Yamphudin Villages

Experience authentic Tibetan Buddhist culture in Ghunsa and rich multi-ethnic Limbu traditions in Yamphudin.

Ilam Tea Gardens & Sub-Tropical Gorges

Drive through emerald tea hills in eastern Nepal and trek through subtropical cardamom and bamboo ravines.

KCA Wilderness Sanctuary

Protected habitat of the endangered Snow Leopard, Red Panda, Blue Sheep, and Himalayan Black Bear.

24-Day Expedition Blueprint

24-Day Grand Kanchenjunga Double Circuit Detailed Itinerary

Complete route from Kathmandu through Ilam tea hills, Pangpema North Base Camp (5,143m), Sele La Pass (4,480m), Oktang South Base Camp (4,730m), and Yamphudin.

Day 1 Flight Kathmandu to Bhadrapur (91m) & Drive to Ilam Tea Gardens (1,600m)
1,600m 45m Flight + 4 hrs Drive +

Take a morning flight from Kathmandu to Bhadrapur in eastern Nepal. Meet your private 4WD driver and travel through rolling subtropical hills to the tea capital of Nepal, Ilam (1,600m). Overnight at a tea estate hotel.

Day 2 4WD Mountain Drive Ilam to Taplejung / Suketar (2,420m)
2,420m 6.5 hrs 4WD Drive +

Drive along the Mechi Highway passing Phidim and climbing into the hills of Taplejung to reach Suketar (2,420m). Meet your porter team and complete final expedition gear arrangements.

Day 3 Trek Suketar to Mitlung (921m) along Tamor River
921m 5 hrs / 11 km +

Descend through terraced rice fields and dark green cardamom plantations towards the roaring Tamor River, reaching the riverside village of Mitlung (921m).

Day 4 Trek Mitlung to Chirwa (1,270m) through Limbu & Rai Villages
1,270m 5.5 hrs / 13 km +

Follow the Tamor River upstream past Limbu villages of Sinwa and Tawa. Cross suspension bridges and boulder-strewn riverbeds to reach the bustling market village of Chirwa (1,270m).

Day 5 Trek Chirwa to Sukathum (1,576m) via Ghunsa Khola Confluence
1,576m 5 hrs / 12 km +

Trek past the entry checkpoint of Kanchenjunga Conservation Area at Taplethok. Follow the confluence where the Ghunsa Khola joins the Tamor to camp or lodge at Sukathum (1,576m).

Day 6 Trek Sukathum to Amjilosa (2,308m) through Bamboo Gorges
2,308m 6 hrs / 13 km +

Climb a steep, narrow gorge carved by the Ghunsa Khola. Traverse bamboo and rhododendron forests across stone steps to the high Sherpa hamlet of Amjilosa (2,308m).

Day 7 Trek Amjilosa to Gyabla / Kyapra (2,730m) past Roaring Waterfalls
2,730m 5 hrs / 10 km +

Trek through dense oak, rhododendron, and fir forests along the river canyon, passing roaring waterfalls to climb up to the Tibetan-influenced village of Gyabla (2,730m).

Day 8 Trek Gyabla to Ghunsa (3,595m) — Tibetan Settlement
3,595m 5.5 hrs / 12 km +

Follow the river as the valley widens. Walk through pine and larch woodlands to Ghunsa (3,595m), the largest and most picturesque Tibetan settlement in the Kanchenjunga region.

Day 9 Acclimatization & Cultural Exploration in Ghunsa (3,595m)
3,595m Acclimatization Day +

Crucial acclimatization day. Take an active morning hike towards Lhonak Peak viewpoint (4,100m) for sweeping views of Phole and Ghunsa valleys. Visit the 400-year-old Ghunsa Gompa in the afternoon.

Day 10 Trek Ghunsa to Kambachen (4,050m) along Glacial Moraine
4,050m 6 hrs / 12 km +

Ascend through larch forests and cross boulder fields along the edge of Kanchenjunga Glacier. Enter the alpine pasture of Kambachen (4,050m) with towering views of Mt. Jannu.

Day 11 Acclimatization Hike around Kambachen: Mt. Jannu Viewpoint
4,050m Side Hike (4,500m) +

Second acclimatization day. Hike up the Nuphchu Khola valley towards the sheer cathedral north face of Mt. Jannu / Kumbhakarna (7,710m).

Day 12 Trek Kambachen to Lhonak (4,780m) across Glacial Scree
4,780m 5.5 hrs / 11 km +

Climb through rocky moraines and cross the Ramtang Glacier basin. Emerge onto the high, windswept sandy plateau of Lhonak (4,780m) surrounded by Wedge Peak and Tent Peak.

Day 13 🏔️ Trek Lhonak to Pangpema North Base Camp (5,143m)
Pangpema 5,143m 6.5 hrs / 12 km +

Early morning trek along the northern lateral moraine of Kanchenjunga Glacier. Arrive at Pangpema North Base Camp (5,143m) directly facing the colossal 3,000m north face of Mt. Kanchenjunga (8,586m), Yalung Kang, and Nepal Peak.

Day 14 Sunrise over Kanchenjunga North Face & Return to Ghunsa (3,595m)
3,595m 7 hrs / 18 km +

Watch golden morning light strike Kanchenjunga. Descend past Lhonak and Kambachen to Ghunsa (3,595m) to prepare for the high pass crossing.

Day 15 Trek Ghunsa to Sele La High Camp (4,290m)
4,290m 4.5 hrs / 8 km +

Ascend through dense mossy forests of juniper, rhododendron, and birch to reach the isolated wilderness huts at Sele La High Camp (4,290m).

Day 16 🏔️ Cross Sele La (4,480m), Sinion La & Mirgin La to Cheram (3,870m)
Sele La 4,480m 7.5 hrs / 14 km +

Spectacular high-altitude traverse crossing Sele La Pass (4,480m), Sinion La (4,440m), and Mirgin La (4,480m) with sweeping views of Mt. Jannu, Kabru, and distant Mt. Makalu (8,485m). Descend into the Yalung Valley at Cheram (3,870m).

Day 17 Trek Cheram to Ramche (4,580m) along Yalung Glacier
4,580m 4.5 hrs / 8 km +

Trek past the snout of the Yalung Glacier alongside frozen streams to the high pasture of Ramche (4,580m) surrounded by blue sheep herds.

Day 18 🏔️ Hike to Oktang South Base Camp (4,730m) & Return to Cheram (3,870m)
Oktang 4,730m 6.5 hrs / 14 km +

Morning hike along the glacial moraine to Oktang Viewpoint (4,730m) directly facing the towering South Face of Mt. Kanchenjunga (8,586m) and the Kabru range. Return down the valley to Cheram (3,870m).

Day 19 Trek Cheram to Tortong (2,995m) down Simbua Valley
2,995m 5 hrs / 11 km +

Descend steadily along the Simbua Khola through dense rhododendron and birch forests back to Tortong (2,995m).

Day 20 Trek Tortong to Yamphudin (1,690m) via Lasiya Bhanjyang (3,310m)
1,690m 6.5 hrs / 13 km +

Climb over Lasiya Bhanjyang ridge (3,310m) and descend through terraced hillsides to the welcoming village of Yamphudin (1,690m).

Day 21 Trek Yamphudin to Khebang (1,915m) / 4WD Pickup Point
1,915m 6 hrs / 12 km +

Final day of trekking. Walk along terraced fields and cardamom farms to the roadhead at Khebang (1,915m). Meet your private 4WD Scorpio jeep crew.

Day 22 Buffer Day / Scenic 4WD Drive to Ilam Tea Resort
1,600m Scenic Drive +

Buffer day for weather flexibility. Drive through the hills to a tea resort in Ilam to relax among emerald tea gardens.

Day 23 Drive to Bhadrapur Airport & Flight to Kathmandu (1,400m)
1,400m Flight to KTM +

Transfer to Bhadrapur airport and take a flight back to Kathmandu. Transfer to your hotel for rest and celebration.

Day 24 Final Farewell Celebration in Kathmandu & Departure
1,400m Celebration +

Farewell dinner in Kathmandu with our team and presentation of your Grand Kanchenjunga Double Circuit expedition certificate.

1. Dual Base Camps: Pangpema (5,143m) & Oktang (4,730m) +

The Grand Circuit visits both the 3,000m north face from Pangpema and the dramatic south face from Oktang, offering the most comprehensive perspective of any 8,000-meter mountain in the Himalayas.

2. Crossing Sele La (4,480m) & Mirgin La (4,480m) +

The high-altitude traverse between Ghunsa and Cheram crosses three distinct passes—Sele La (4,480m), Sinion La (4,440m), and Mirgin La (4,480m)—with sweeping views of Jannu and Makalu.

3. Mt. Jannu / Kumbhakarna (7,710m) Cathedral Wall +

Viewed from Kambachen and Sele La, Mt. Jannu presents a sheer vertical granite wall considered one of the steepest technical mountaineering challenges in the world.

4. Tibetan Heritage in Ghunsa Village (3,595m) +

Ghunsa is a stone-built village settled by Tibetan refugees centuries ago. It features traditional wooden shingle roofs, prayer flag poles, ancient gompas, and welcoming teahouses.

5. Restricted Area Permit (RAP) Regulations +

Kanchenjunga is a protected restricted area requiring a minimum of 2 trekkers, an authorized agency, a licensed guide, and Special Restricted Area Permits ($20 USD/week) + KCA entry fees (all included).

6. 24-Day Physical Stamina & Conditioning +

This is a demanding 24-day expedition. Pre-trip training should emphasize endurance cardio, hill climbing with weighted packs, and leg strength for consecutive walking days.

7. Eastern Nepal Logistics: Flights & 4WD Jeeps +

We combine flights to Bhadrapur or Suketar with private 4WD Scorpio jeeps through Ilam tea country to avoid unreliable mountain roads and minimize delays.

8. Teahouses and High Wilderness Lodges +

Lodges along the route are simple, authentic, and cozy with warm dining room hearths. In Lhonak, Pangpema, and Sele La, accommodation is in rustic mountain shelters.

9. Ilam Rolling Tea Estates Experience +

Driving through Ilam provides views of lush green orthodox tea plantations reminiscent of Darjeeling, providing an emerald contrast to high glacial mountains.

10. High-Altitude Medical Kit & Oxygen +

Your guide carries pulse oximeters, emergency medical oxygen, Diamox, and Garmin inReach satellite communication. Helipads are available at Ghunsa, Yamphudin, and Taplejung.

11. Best Weather Seasons (Spring vs Autumn) +

Spring (March–May) brings blooming rhododendron forests and mild days. Autumn (September–November) offers crystal clear visibility and stable mountain weather for crossing Sele La.

12. Flexible 15% Deposit Guarantee +

What is the difference between Kanchenjunga Circuit and North/South only? +

The Grand Circuit connects BOTH North Base Camp (Pangpema 5,143m) and South Base Camp (Oktang 4,730m) by crossing Sele La Pass (4,480m), offering a complete 24-day traverse of the mountain massif.

What permits are required for the Grand Kanchenjunga Circuit? +

You require the Kanchenjunga Restricted Area Permit (RAP, $20 USD per person per week) and the Kanchenjunga Conservation Area Project (KCAP) entry permit. Both permits are 100% arranged and included in your package.

Can solo trekkers join the Kanchenjunga Circuit? +

By Nepal Department of Immigration regulations, a minimum of 2 trekkers is required to issue the Restricted Area Permit. Solo trekkers can join our guaranteed fixed departure groups.

How difficult is crossing Sele La Pass (4,480m)? +

The pass day is a 7.5-hour trek across Sele La (4,480m), Sinion La (4,440m), and Mirgin La (4,480m). It is a non-technical alpine trail, but requires good stamina and microspikes if there is spring snow.

What accommodation is available on the 24-day trek? +

You stay in comfortable village teahouses in Suketar, Chirwa, Ghunsa, Yamphudin, and Cheram. In high-altitude Lhonak, Pangpema, and Sele La, accommodation is in rustic stone mountain shelters with warm dining hearths.

Are all domestic flights and 4WD jeeps included? +

Yes! The package includes flights Kathmandu ⇄ Bhadrapur/Suketar, private rugged 4WD Scorpio jeep transfers through Ilam, and airport transfers in Kathmandu.

How difficult is the 24-day Grand Circuit? +

It is graded Extreme (5/5) due to its 24-day duration, remote wilderness setting, high pass crossing, and two base camp visits. Excellent physical and mental endurance are required.

How is altitude sickness prevented on this route? +

The trek takes 13 full days to reach Pangpema (5,143m) with 2 dedicated acclimatization days in Ghunsa (3,595m) and Kambachen (4,050m). Your guide conducts twice-daily pulse oximetry monitoring.

When is the best time to trek the Grand Circuit? +

Spring (March to May) and Autumn (September to November) are prime. Spring offers vibrant rhododendron forests, while Autumn provides the clearest skies and most stable pass conditions.

How does the 15% flexible deposit work? +

You pay 15% ($434) online to lock in your flight reservations, restricted permits, and Sherpa team. The remaining 85% is paid upon arrival in Kathmandu. Deposits feature lifetime validity protection.

What equipment is provided free by Best Treks Nepal? +

We provide a complimentary 100L waterproof duffel bag, high-grade -20°C expedition down sleeping bag, and branded trek shirt upon your arrival in Kathmandu.

Is emergency helicopter evacuation available in Kanchenjunga? +

Yes. Helicopter rescue coordinates can be dispatched directly to Ghunsa, Cheram, Yamphudin, and Taplejung in coordination with your emergency travel insurance.
