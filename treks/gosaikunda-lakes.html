Holy Alpine Waters

The Sacred Gosaikunda Lakes Experience

Revered by Hindus and Buddhists alike, the Sacred Gosaikunda Lakes Trek takes you on an unforgettable journey to a cluster of over 108 glacial alpine tarns perched at 4,380m inside Langtang National Park. According to ancient Hindu mythology, Lord Shiva created these high lakes when he pierced the mountain with his holy trident (Trishul) to draw cold water and cool his burning throat after consuming the cosmic poison (Halahala).

Starting from the district hub of Dhunche (2,030m), you ascend through dense rhododendron and pine forests up to Sing Gompa (3,330m), home to a sacred monastery and artisanal yak cheese factory. Climbing further along the open spine of Laurebina Ridge (3,910m), you are treated to magnificent sunsets with views stretching across Ganesh Himal, Manaslu, Annapurna, and Langtang Lirung. Continuing past Saraswati Kunda and Bhairav Kunda, you reach the pristine shores of Gosaikunda Lake (4,380m) and can hike to the panoramic vantage point of Laurebina Pass (4,610m).

Holy Lake Trail Blueprint

7-Day Sacred Gosaikunda Detailed Itinerary

Journey step-by-step from Kathmandu through Dhunche, climbing the scenic ridge via Sing Gompa and Laurebina to Sacred Gosaikunda Lake (4,380m) and Laurebina Pass (4,610m).

Day 1 Scenic Private 4WD Jeep Drive from Kathmandu to Dhunche (2,030m)
2,030m 5–6 hrs Drive +

Morning private 4WD Scorpio jeep pickup from your Kathmandu hotel. Drive northwest along the scenic Trishuli River highway through Kakani and Trishuli Bazar, climbing into the pine-clad hills of Rasuwa district. Check into your lodge in Dhunche (2,030m), the administrative headquarters of Langtang National Park.

Sherpa Drive Tip: Dhunche is the gateway to Gosaikunda. We check permits at the park entrance gate and enjoy an evening briefing on high-altitude hydration.
Day 2 Trek Dhunche to Sing Gompa / Chandanbari (3,330m) via Deurali
3,330m 5 hrs / 9 km +

Cross the Trishuli Khola and climb steadily through lush oak, hemlock, and rhododendron forests past Ghatte Khola and Deurali (2,660m). Emerge at the peaceful ridge settlement of Sing Gompa (3,330m). Visit the ancient Buddhist monastery and tour the local artisan yak cheese factory.

Day 3 Trek Sing Gompa to Laurebina Yak Ridge (3,910m)
3,910m 3.5 hrs / 6 km +

Climb past the tree line onto the open, panoramic spine of Laurebina ridge (3,910m). The trail provides jaw-dropping vistas of the Ganesh Himal (7,422m), Manaslu, Annapurna, and Tibetan border peaks. Watch the fiery sunset paint the snow peaks in crimson alpenglow.

Sherpa Sunset Tip: Laurebina is considered one of the finest sunset viewpoints in central Nepal. Sleeping here at 3,910m gives your body ideal acclimatization before reaching Gosaikunda.
Day 4 Trek Laurebina to Sacred Gosaikunda Lake (4,380m) & Explore Holy Lakes
4,380m ⭐ Holy Lake Day +

Follow the high cliff trail past small shrines, passing first the tranquil waters of Saraswati Kunda and then the deep black tarn of Bhairav Kunda. Emerge at the majestic turquoise basin of Sacred Gosaikunda Lake (4,380m). Check into your lakeside lodge, visit the Shiva shrine, and stroll around the sacred lakeshore under towering granite walls.

Day 5 Sunrise at Surya Peak / Laurebina Pass (4,610m) & Descend to Sing Gompa (3,330m)
4,610m ⭐ High Pass +

Early morning hike past Surya Kunda up to the panoramic ridge of Laurebina Pass (4,610m). Savor 360° views across the Himalayas. After soaking in the sunrise, descend past Gosaikunda and Laurebina down into the sheltered forests at Sing Gompa (3,330m) for a warm celebratory dinner.

Sherpa Pass Advice: The morning ascent to Laurebina Pass viewpoint takes ~2 hours from Gosaikunda. The descent back down to Sing Gompa feels effortless as oxygen levels rapidly increase.
Day 6 Trek Sing Gompa through Pine Forests down to Dhunche (2,030m)
2,030m 4 hrs / 8 km +

Descend gently through aromatic pine, fir, and oak woodlands past Deurali and Dimsa. Cross the suspension bridge over Trishuli Khola back to Dhunche. Celebrate the completion of the pilgrimage trek with your Sherpa guide and team.

Day 7 Private 4WD Jeep Drive Dhunche to Kathmandu (1,400m) & Farewell Dinner
1,400m 5–6 hrs Drive +

Board our private 4WD Scorpio jeep for the scenic return drive along the foothills back to Kathmandu. Check into your hotel and join us for an evening Nepali farewell dinner and cultural performance.

1. Religious & Mythological Significance of Gosaikunda +

Gosaikunda is revered as the sacred lake created by Lord Shiva's trident (Trishul). Hindu and Buddhist pilgrims believe bathing in the holy waters cleanses sins and grants liberation.

2. Janai Purnima Full Moon Festival (August) +

During the full moon of August, thousands of pilgrims and shamans (Jhankris) in traditional ceremonial attire trek to the lake for spiritual cleansing and sacred thread renewal.

3. Altitude & Acclimatization Staging +

You ascend from Dhunche (2,030m) to Sing Gompa (3,330m) and Laurebina (3,910m) before reaching Gosaikunda (4,380m). This gradual climb allows your body to acclimatize smoothly.

4. Laurebina Pass (4,610m) Crossing to Helambu +

Our standard 7-day trek visits the pass viewpoint at sunrise. Trekkers with more time can cross over Laurebina Pass into the Helambu valley and exit at Sundarijal (10–12 Days).

5. Permits Included (Langtang National Park & TIMS) +

Langtang National Park entry permit and TIMS card are fully included and processed by our Kathmandu office prior to departure.

6. Sing Gompa & Chandanbari Yak Cheese Factory +

At Sing Gompa (3,330m), you can explore the Buddhist monastery and purchase freshly made Nak cheese directly from the high-altitude dairy.

7. Road Travel via Private 4WD Jeep +

The 115km drive between Kathmandu and Dhunche takes 5 to 6 hours. We exclusively provide private Mahindra Scorpio 4WD jeeps with seasoned mountain drivers.

8. Temperature & Layering (-6°C to 20°C) +

Daytime walking in the forests is warm (15°C to 20°C), while nights at Gosaikunda Lake (4,380m) drop between -2°C and -6°C. A -15°C down sleeping bag is provided free.

9. Food & Mountain Nutrition (Full Board Included) +

Breakfast, lunch, and dinner are included. Menus feature hot Dal Bhat, garlic noodle soup, potato pancakes, artisan yak cheese omelets, and ginger lemon tea.

10. Holy Lake Etiquette & Conservation +

Gosaikunda is a sacred Ramsar Wetland site. Please respect local religious sensitivities, do not use soaps in the water, and follow Leave No Trace principles.

11. Medical Safety & Oxygen Monitoring +

Our guides carry medical pulse oximeters and first aid kits. Helipad emergency rescue is available at Gosaikunda and Sing Gompa for immediate response if needed.

12. Flexible 15% Deposit & Rescheduling +

Frequently Asked Questions

Direct answers from our senior trail team covering Gosaikunda lake logistics, altitude precautions, and sacred etiquette.

Showing all 12 questions
Can trekkers bathe or swim in Sacred Gosaikunda Lake? +

Pilgrims perform ritual spiritual dips along the designated stone ghats. Casual recreational swimming or using soap/shampoo is strictly prohibited to preserve the sacred purity and ecology of the Ramsar wetland.

How difficult is the 7-day Gosaikunda Lakes Trek? +

The trek is moderately challenging with 4 to 5 hours of uphill hiking per day. Staging the ascent with overnight stays at Sing Gompa and Laurebina makes the climb comfortable for anyone with average fitness.

How many lakes are there around Gosaikunda? +

The glacial basin contains 108 alpine lakes (Kundas). The primary accessible ones along our route are Saraswati Kunda, Bhairav Kunda, Gosaikunda, Surya Kunda, and Chandra Kunda.

How long is the private 4WD drive from Kathmandu to Dhunche? +

The 115km drive takes approximately 5 to 6 hours in our private Mahindra Scorpio 4WD jeep, offering comfortable travel through Trishuli and foothill valleys.

What are the teahouse facilities like at Gosaikunda Lake? +

Teahouses at Gosaikunda provide cozy twin rooms, warm quilts, central wood-heated dining rooms, and hot food. Rooms have incredible views overlooking the turquoise lake.

Is there risk of altitude sickness on this trek? +

Because you spend nights at 2,030m, 3,330m, and 3,910m before sleeping at 4,380m, acclimatization is gradual. Our guides perform twice-daily pulse oximetry monitoring.

How does the 15% deposit policy work? +

You pay a 15% deposit ($98) online to secure your trek departure and permits. The remaining 85% is paid upon arrival in Nepal. Deposits have lifetime validity with free date changes up to 30 days prior.

What meals are included during the trek? +

Full board (Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner) is included on trek. Menus feature Dal Bhat, local Nak cheese dishes, potato soup, Tibetan bread, pasta, momos, and hot ginger honey tea.

Can Gosaikunda be combined with Langtang Valley? +

Yes! You can easily combine the Langtang Valley trek (Kyanjin Ri) with Gosaikunda Lakes via Thulo Syabru (14 Days total). Contact our team for custom combinations.

What permits are included in the package? +

Both the Langtang National Park entry permit and TIMS (Trekkers' Information Management System) card are fully included and processed by our Kathmandu office before departure.

What is the story behind Lord Shiva and Gosaikunda? +

During the churning of the ocean (Samudra Manthan), Shiva swallowed the deadly poison Halahala to save the universe. In unbearable agony, he struck the mountain with his Trishul to release the pure waters of Gosaikunda to quench his thirst.

What gear is provided free by Best Treks Nepal? +

We provide a complimentary 100L waterproof duffel bag, an expedition-grade -15°C down sleeping bag, and a branded trekking shirt upon your arrival in Kathmandu.
