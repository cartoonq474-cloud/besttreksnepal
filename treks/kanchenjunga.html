The Great Eastern Wilderness

Kanchenjunga North Base Camp Trek (21 Days)

The Kanchenjunga North Base Camp Trek (21 Days) is one of the most untouched and awe-inspiring trekking expeditions in the Nepal Himalaya. Leading deep into the protected Kanchenjunga Conservation Area (KCA) bordering Sikkim and Tibet, this route culminates at Pangpema (5,143m), directly at the foot of the mammoth 3,000-meter north face of Mt. Kanchenjunga (8,586m), the third highest mountain on Earth.

Starting from the rolling green tea plantations of Ilam and sub-tropical cardamom forests along the Tamor River, the trek transitions through bamboo ravines, dense rhododendron woodlands, and high alpine glacial valleys. Along the way, you will immerse yourself in the rich Tibetan Buddhist traditions of Ghunsa (3,595m), gaze at the sheer vertical rock wall of Mt. Jannu / Kumbhakarna (7,710m), and cross glacial moraines to the remote outpost of Lhonak (4,780m).

North Face of Mt. Kanchenjunga (8,586m)

Stand face-to-face with the immense 3,000-meter wall of ice and rock dominating the Pangpema amphitheater.

Mt. Jannu / Kumbhakarna (7,710m)

Marvel at one of the world's most formidable alpine cathedral walls rising vertically from Kambachen.

Ghunsa Tibetan Settlement (3,595m)

Explore stone houses, juniper incense hearths, and ancient monasteries preserving Tibetan refugee heritage.

Lhonak Glacial Basin (4,780m)

Stay in stone shelters surrounded by massive moraine ridges, blue sheep herds, and Wedge Peak views.

Ilam Tea Gardens & Limbu Culture

Drive through emerald tea hills in eastern Nepal and trek through authentic Limbu and Rai villages.

KCA Wildlife Sanctuary

Habitat of the endangered Snow Leopard, Red Panda, Himalayan Black Bear, and Blood Pheasant.

21-Day Expedition Blueprint

21-Day Kanchenjunga North Base Camp Detailed Itinerary

Complete route from Kathmandu through Ilam tea hills, along the Tamor and Ghunsa river gorges, to Pangpema North Base Camp (5,143m) and back.

Day 1 Flight Kathmandu to Bhadrapur (91m) & Drive to Ilam Tea Gardens (1,600m)
1,600m 45m Flight + 4 hrs Drive +

Take a morning flight from Kathmandu to Bhadrapur in eastern Nepal. Meet your private 4WD driver and travel through rolling subtropical hills to the tea capital of Nepal, Ilam (1,600m). Overnight at a tea estate hotel.

Day 2 4WD Mountain Drive Ilam to Taplejung / Suketar (2,420m)
2,420m 6.5 hrs 4WD Drive +

Drive along the Mechi Highway passing Phidim and climbing into the hills of Taplejung to reach Suketar (2,420m). Meet your porter team and complete final expedition gear arrangements.

Day 3 Trek Suketar to Mitlung (921m) through Cardamom Fields
921m 5 hrs / 11 km +

Descend through terraced rice fields and dark green cardamom plantations towards the roaring Tamor River, reaching the riverside village of Mitlung (921m).

Day 4 Trek Mitlung to Chirwa (1,270m) through Limbu & Rai Villages
1,270m 5.5 hrs / 13 km +

Follow the Tamor River upstream past Limbu villages of Sinwa and Tawa. Cross suspension bridges and boulder-strewn riverbeds to reach the bustling market village of Chirwa (1,270m).

Day 5 Trek Chirwa to Sukathum (1,576m) via Ghunsa Khola Confluence
1,576m 5 hrs / 12 km +

Trek past the entry checkpoint of Kanchenjunga Conservation Area at Taplethok. Follow the confluence where the Ghunsa Khola joins the Tamor to camp or lodge at Sukathum (1,576m).

Day 6 Trek Sukathum to Amjilosa (2,308m) through Bamboo Gorges
2,308m 6 hrs / 13 km +

Climb a steep, narrow gorge carved by the Ghunsa Khola. Traverse bamboo and rhododendron forests across stone steps to the high Sherpa hamlet of Amjilosa (2,308m).

Day 7 Trek Amjilosa to Gyabla / Kyapra (2,730m) past Roaring Waterfalls
2,730m 5 hrs / 10 km +

Trek through dense oak, rhododendron, and fir forests along the river canyon, passing roaring waterfalls to climb up to the Tibetan-influenced village of Gyabla (2,730m).

Day 8 Trek Gyabla to Ghunsa (3,595m) — Tibetan Refugee Village
Ghunsa 3,595m 5.5 hrs / 12 km +

Follow the river as the valley widens. Walk through pine and larch woodlands to Ghunsa (3,595m), the largest and most picturesque Tibetan settlement in the Kanchenjunga region.

Day 9 Acclimatization & Cultural Exploration in Ghunsa (3,595m)
3,595m Acclimatization Day +

Crucial acclimatization day. Take an active morning hike towards Lhonak Peak viewpoint (4,100m) for sweeping views of Phole and Ghunsa valleys. In the afternoon, visit the 400-year-old Ghunsa Gompa and local carpet weaving cooperatives.

Day 10 Trek Ghunsa to Kambachen (4,050m) along Glacial Moraine
4,050m 6 hrs / 12 km +

Ascend through larch forests and cross boulder fields along the edge of Kanchenjunga Glacier. Enter the alpine pasture of Kambachen (4,050m) with towering views of Mt. Jannu.

Day 11 Acclimatization Hike around Kambachen: Mt. Jannu Viewpoint
4,050m Side Hike (4,500m) +

Second acclimatization day before pushing past 5,000 meters. Hike up the Nuphchu Khola valley towards the sheer cathedral north face of Mt. Jannu / Kumbhakarna (7,710m).

Day 12 Trek Kambachen to Lhonak (4,780m) across Glacial Scree
4,780m 5.5 hrs / 11 km +

Climb through rocky moraines and cross the Ramtang Glacier basin. Emerge onto the high, windswept sandy plateau of Lhonak (4,780m) surrounded by Wedge Peak and Tent Peak.

Day 13 🏔️ Trek Lhonak to Pangpema North Base Camp (5,143m)
Pangpema 5,143m 6.5 hrs / 12 km +

Early morning trek along the northern lateral moraine of Kanchenjunga Glacier. Arrive at Pangpema North Base Camp (5,143m) directly facing the colossal 3,000m north face of Mt. Kanchenjunga (8,586m), Yalung Kang, and Nepal Peak.

Ridge Viewpoint (5,380m): Hike 200m up the grassy ridge behind the Pangpema stone hut for an unobstructed 180° panorama of the entire Kanchenjunga massif into Tibet.
Day 14 Sunrise over Kanchenjunga North Face & Return to Lhonak (4,780m)
4,780m 4 hrs / 8 km +

Watch the first golden rays of sunlight illuminate the immense summit of Kanchenjunga. After photography and tea, retrace your steps along the glacial moraine back to Lhonak (4,780m).

Day 15 Trek Lhonak to Ghunsa (3,595m) via Kambachen
3,595m 6 hrs / 15 km +

Descend from the glacial plateau past Kambachen to the rich oxygen and warm hospitality of Ghunsa (3,595m). Enjoy hot tea and a traditional Sherpa meal.

Day 16 Trek Ghunsa to Amjilosa (2,308m)
2,308m 6.5 hrs / 17 km +

Descend through birch and rhododendron forests past Gyabla to the hillside settlement of Amjilosa (2,308m).

Day 17 Trek Amjilosa to Chirwa (1,270m) along Ghunsa Khola
1,270m 6 hrs / 16 km +

Follow the river gorges down to Sukathum and continue along the Tamor River to the sub-tropical village of Chirwa (1,270m).

Day 18 Trek Chirwa to Suketar / Taplejung (2,420m)
2,420m 6.5 hrs / 17 km +

Final day of trekking. Climb through Limbu villages and terraced farms back up to the ridge at Suketar (2,420m). Celebrate the trek with your guide and porter crew.

Day 19 Buffer Day / Scenic 4WD Drive to Birtamod or Ilam
1,600m Contingency Drive +

Buffer day for weather flexibility. Drive through the hills to Birtamod or stay at a tea resort in Ilam.

Day 20 Drive to Bhadrapur Airport & Flight back to Kathmandu (1,400m)
1,400m Flight to KTM +

Transfer to Bhadrapur airport and take a flight back to Kathmandu. Transfer to your hotel for rest and celebration.

Day 21 Final Farewell Celebration in Kathmandu & Departure
1,400m Celebration +

Farewell dinner in Kathmandu with our team and presentation of your Kanchenjunga North Base Camp expedition certificate.

Essential preparation on Pangpema (5,143m), Mt. Jannu, restricted area permits, and 21-day expedition conditioning.

1. Standing at Pangpema North Base Camp (5,143m) +

Pangpema offers one of the most sheer mountain panoramas in the Himalayas. The north face of Kanchenjunga rises 3,500 vertical meters above the glacier. The viewpoint ridge behind camp reaches 5,380m.

2. Mt. Jannu / Kumbhakarna (7,710m) Cathedral Face +

Viewed from Kambachen (4,050m), Mt. Jannu presents a vertical granite wall considered one of the steepest technical mountaineering challenges in the world, reverently worshipped by local Kirat and Limbu people.

3. North Base Camp vs South Base Camp (Oktang) +

The North Base Camp (Pangpema) route is generally favored for its closer proximity to the main peak wall, dramatic glacial terrain, and Tibetan settlements of Ghunsa, compared to South Base Camp.

4. Tibetan Heritage in Ghunsa Village (3,595m) +

Ghunsa is a stone-built village settled by Tibetan refugees centuries ago. It features traditional wooden shingle roofs, prayer flag poles, ancient gompas, and welcoming teahouses.

5. Restricted Area Permit (RAP) Rules +

Kanchenjunga is a designated restricted area requiring a minimum of 2 trekkers, an authorized agency, a licensed guide, and Special Restricted Area Permits ($20 USD/week) + KCA entry fees (all included).

6. 21-Day Endurance & Acclimatization Strategy +

With 2 planned acclimatization days in Ghunsa (3,595m) and Kambachen (4,050m), the itinerary gains altitude gradually over 13 days to ensure safe progression to 5,143m.

7. Eastern Nepal Logistics: Flights & 4WD Jeeps +

We combine flights to Bhadrapur or Suketar with private 4WD Scorpio jeeps through Ilam tea country to avoid unreliable mountain roads and minimize delays.

8. Wilderness Teahouse Accommodations +

Lodges along the route are simple, authentic, and cozy with warm dining room hearths. In Lhonak and Pangpema, accommodation is in stone mountain shelters.

9. Ilam Rolling Tea Estates Experience +

Driving through Ilam offers views of lush green orthodox tea plantations reminiscent of Darjeeling, providing a tropical contrast to high glacial mountains.

10. High-Altitude Medical Kit & Oxygen +

Your guide carries pulse oximeters, emergency medical oxygen, Diamox, and Garmin inReach satellite communication. Helipads are available at Ghunsa and Taplejung.

11. Best Weather Seasons (Spring vs Autumn) +

Spring (March–May) brings blooming rhododendron forests and mild days. Autumn (September–November) offers crystal clear visibility and stable mountain weather.

Essential Clarifications

Frequently Asked Questions

Direct answers regarding Pangpema Base Camp access, permits, physical fitness, teahouse lodges, and flights.

What is the view from Pangpema North Base Camp (5,143m)? +

Pangpema sits directly beneath the 3,000-meter vertical wall of Mt. Kanchenjunga (8,586m), with views of Yalung Kang (8,505m), Kangbachen Peak (7,903m), and Nepal Peak. Hiking to the 5,380m ridge offers an unobstructed 180° panorama.

What permits are required for Kanchenjunga North Base Camp? +

You require the Kanchenjunga Restricted Area Permit (RAP, $20 USD per person per week) and the Kanchenjunga Conservation Area Project (KCAP) entry permit. Both permits are 100% arranged and included in your package.

Can solo trekkers do the Kanchenjunga trek? +

By Nepal Department of Immigration regulations, a minimum of 2 trekkers is required to issue the Restricted Area Permit. Solo trekkers can join our guaranteed fixed departure groups.

What accommodation is available on the 21-day trek? +

You stay in comfortable teahouses in Suketar, Chirwa, Amjilosa, Ghunsa, and Kambachen. In high-altitude Lhonak and Pangpema, accommodation is in rustic stone mountain lodges with warm dining hearths.

Are all domestic flights and 4WD jeeps included? +

Yes! The package includes flights Kathmandu ⇄ Bhadrapur/Suketar, private rugged 4WD Scorpio jeep transfers through Ilam, and airport transfers in Kathmandu.

How difficult is the 21-day Kanchenjunga trek? +

It is graded Strenuous (4/5) due to its 21-day duration and remote wilderness setting. Daily walking times average 5 to 6.5 hours. Excellent aerobic fitness and mental endurance are required.

How is altitude sickness prevented on this route? +

The trek takes 13 full days to reach Pangpema (5,143m) with 2 dedicated acclimatization days in Ghunsa (3,595m) and Kambachen (4,050m). Your guide conducts daily pulse oximetry monitoring.

When is the best time to trek Kanchenjunga North? +

Spring (March to May) and Autumn (September to November) are prime. Spring offers vibrant rhododendron forests, while Autumn provides the clearest blue skies and sharpest mountain views.

How does the 15% flexible deposit work? +

You pay 15% ($374) online to lock in your flight reservations, restricted permits, and Sherpa team. The remaining 85% is paid upon arrival in Kathmandu. Deposits feature lifetime validity protection.

What is the cultural background of Ghunsa? +

Ghunsa is inhabited by Tibetan Buddhist Sherpa families. The village features traditional wood-shingled houses, yak pens, ancient gompas, and welcoming tea rooms.

What equipment is provided free by Best Treks Nepal? +

We provide a complimentary 100L waterproof duffel bag, high-grade -20°C expedition down sleeping bag, and branded trek shirt upon your arrival in Kathmandu.

Is emergency helicopter evacuation available in Kanchenjunga? +

Yes. Helicopter rescue coordinates can be dispatched directly to Ghunsa, Kambachen, Lhonak, and Taplejung in coordination with your emergency travel insurance.
