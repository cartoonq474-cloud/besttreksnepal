Trip Overview & Experience

The Gokyo Lakes & Gokyo Ri Trek is celebrated by seasoned alpinists as the most scenic and rewarding high-altitude journey in the Everest Khumbu region. Diverging from the busier classic EBC trail above Sanasa, this expedition leads you north through fragrant birch and rhododendron forests into the secluded Gokyo Valley, culminating in six sacred cobalt-turquoise glacial lakes nestled at the foot of Mount Cho Oyu (8,188m) and the massive Ngozumpa Glacier.

Ascending to the summit of Gokyo Ri (5,357m / 17,575 ft) at sunrise offers an unmatched 360-degree panorama of four of the world’s six tallest 8,000-meter peaks: Mount Everest (8,848.86m), Lhotse (8,516m), Makalu (8,485m), and Cho Oyu (8,188m). With serene trails, authentic Sherpa hospitality in uncrowded teahouse lodges, and dramatic glacial terrain, this trek is the quintessential Himalayan adventure.

14-Day Detailed Itinerary

·
01

Fly Kathmandu / Ramechhap to Lukla (2,840m) & Trek to Phakding

2,610m (Phakding) 3.5–4 hrs 8 km (5.0 mi) Lunch & Dinner

Early morning transfer to the domestic airport for your breathtaking 35-minute mountain flight to Tenzing-Hillary Airport in Lukla (2,840m), soaring over alpine valleys and terraced foothills. Upon touchdown on Lukla's famous inclined runway, meet your dedicated native Sherpa porter team and enjoy a hot breakfast. We begin trekking along the gentle downhill trail following the milky glacial waters of the Dudh Koshi River, passing carved Mani stone boulders, fluttering prayer flags, and peaceful stone-walled farming hamlets to reach Phakding.

  • Key Sights: Thrilling landing at Lukla mountain airstrip, Kusum Kanguru (6,367m) peak view
  • Overnight: Mountain Teahouse Lodge in Phakding (Twin Room)
  • Meals Provided: Lunch, Dinner (Hot tea included)
02

Trek Phakding to Namche Bazaar (3,440m) via Hillary Suspension Bridge

3,440m (Namche) 5.5–6 hrs 11 km (6.8 mi) Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

Following the turquoise Dudh Koshi riverbank, we cross several thrilling suspension bridges draped with Buddhist prayer flags. We enter the official UNESCO-designated Sagarmatha National Park checkpoint at Monjo and stop for lunch at Jorsalle. After crossing the iconic Hillary Suspension Bridge high above the river confluence, we begin the sustained switchback climb through fragrant pine and blue pine forests. Halfway up, catch your very first sight of Mount Everest peeking through the trees before reaching the bustling Sherpa capital of Namche Bazaar.

  • Key Sights: Hillary Suspension Bridge, first glimpse of Mt. Everest & Lhotse
  • Overnight: Comfortable Alpine Lodge in Namche Bazaar
  • Meals Provided: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner
03

Acclimatization Day in Namche: Hike to Hotel Everest View (3,880m) & Khumjung

3,880m (Viewpoint) 4–5 hrs Active Rest 6 km (3.7 mi) Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

Crucial "climb high, sleep low" acclimatization excursion. We hike above Namche past the Syangboche airstrip to the world-renowned Hotel Everest View (3,880m). Here, enjoy freshly brewed Himalayan coffee on the panoramic patio with unobstructed sightlines of Everest, Ama Dablam, Lhotse, Nuptse, and Thamserku. We continue down to the traditional green-roofed village of Khumjung to visit the ancient monastery preserving the famous Yeti scalp and the Sir Edmund Hillary School.

  • Key Sights: Grand panoramic view of Ama Dablam, Khumjung Monastery & Hillary School
  • Overnight: Comfortable Alpine Lodge in Namche Bazaar
  • Meals Provided: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner
04

Trek Namche Bazaar to Mong La Pass (3,975m) & Dole (4,200m)

4,200m (Dole) 5.5–6 hrs 12 km (7.5 mi) Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

Today we bid farewell to the busy classic Everest Base Camp highway at Sanasa, turning north onto the tranquil Gokyo Valley route. We climb up a scenic spur to Mong La Pass (3,975m), the birthplace of the revered Buddhist saint Lama Sange Dorje, offering a staggering close-up view of Ama Dablam and Kangtega. We descend steeply through rhododendron forest to Phortse Thenga (3,680m) for lunch beside the river, before embarking on a steady uphill climb through silver birch woods to the settlement of Dole.

  • Key Sights: Diverging into tranquil Gokyo Valley, Mong La Pass Chorten, views of Mt. Khumbila
  • Overnight: Mountain Teahouse in Dole
  • Meals Provided: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner
05

Trek Dole to Luza (4,360m) & Machhermo (4,470m)

4,470m (Machhermo) 4.5–5 hrs 7 km (4.3 mi) Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

Leaving Dole, the trail climbs onto an alpine ridge high above the canyon where juniper and alpine shrubs replace forests. We pass through high summer yak grazing settlements at Lhabarma and Luza (4,360m), enjoying majestic views of Cho Oyu (8,188m), Kantega, and Thamserku. The trail contours along the northern valley wall before dropping into the broad glacial basin of Machhermo. In the afternoon, we attend the daily high-altitude medical briefing at the Himalayan Rescue Association (HRA) porter clinic.

  • Key Sights: First majestic view of Cho Oyu (8,188m), Machhermo Porter Clinic briefing
  • Overnight: Alpine Lodge in Machhermo
  • Meals Provided: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner
06

Trek Machhermo to Gokyo Village & 3rd Sacred Lake (4,790m)

4,790m (Gokyo) 4.5–5 hrs 7 km (4.3 mi) Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

A truly unforgettable day. We ascend the valley through Pangka (where a devastating avalanche struck in 1995) to reach the snout and terminal moraine of the enormous Ngozumpa Glacier. We step onto stone staircases to pass the first emerald lake, Longpongo (4,690m), followed by the second lake, Taboche Tsho (4,740m), where Brahminy ducks swim in glacier water. Finally, we arrive at the third sacred lake, Dudh Pokhari (4,790m), on whose tranquil eastern shores sits the picturesque stone settlement of Gokyo Village.

  • Key Sights: Longpongo (1st Lake), Taboche Tsho (2nd Lake), and stunning cobalt Dudh Pokhari (3rd Lake)
  • Overnight: Lakeside Teahouse in Gokyo Village
  • Meals Provided: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner
07

Pre-Dawn Summit of Gokyo Ri (5,357m) & Excursion to 4th Lake (Thonak Tsho)

5,357m (Gokyo Ri Summit) 6–7 hrs 10 km (6.2 mi) Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

We begin our climb under headlamp at 4:30 AM to ascend the steep western ridge of Gokyo Ri (5,357m). As the first rays of sunlight break over the Great Himalayan divide, marvel at the single most majestic mountain panorama on Earth: Mount Everest (8,848.86m), Lhotse (8,516m), Makalu (8,485m), and Cho Oyu (8,188m) bathed in golden alpenglow, with the massive Ngozumpa Glacier snaking below. We descend to Gokyo for a hearty hot breakfast, followed by an afternoon stroll along the glacial moraine to Thonak Tsho (4th Lake, 4,870m).

  • Key Sights: Summiting Gokyo Ri (5,357m), sunrise on 4 of the world's 6 highest peaks, 4th Lake
  • Overnight: Lakeside Teahouse in Gokyo Village
  • Meals Provided: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner
08

Exploration of 5th Lake (Ngozumpa Tsho), Scoundrel's Viewpoint & Cho Oyu Base Camp View

4,990m (5th Lake) 5.5–6 hrs 11 km (6.8 mi) Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

Today we hike north past the 4th Lake up to Ngozumpa Tsho (5th Lake, 4,990m) and Scoundrel's Viewpoint. From this high knoll on the edge of the glacier moraine, gaze upon the colossal southern wall of Cho Oyu and the north face of Everest with the sprawling ice labyrinth of the Ngozumpa Glacier stretching beneath your feet. In high seasons with strong groups, we can trek further toward the 6th Lake (Gyazumba Tsho) near the Tibet border before returning to Gokyo.

  • Key Sights: Ngozumpa Tsho (5th Lake), Scoundrel's Viewpoint, Cho Oyu South Face & Icefalls
  • Overnight: Lakeside Teahouse in Gokyo Village
  • Meals Provided: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner
09

Trek Gokyo Village down through Pangka & Machhermo to Dole (4,200m)

4,200m (Dole) 5–6 hrs 12 km (7.5 mi) Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

After enjoying a final morning vista of the sun hitting the emerald waters of Dudh Pokhari, we begin our descent. Retracing our steps past the 2nd and 1st lakes, we descend down the terminal moraine of the glacier to Pangka. We continue past Machhermo for lunch, and then follow the scenic high trail above the Dudh Koshi River through Luza and Lhabarma back down to the sheltered hamlet of Dole.

  • Key Sights: Easy downhill pacing, rich oxygen restoration, panoramic views of Thamserku
  • Overnight: Mountain Teahouse in Dole
  • Meals Provided: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner
10

Trek Dole to Phortse Thenga, Mong La Pass & Namche Bazaar (3,440m)

3,440m (Namche) 5–5.5 hrs 11 km (6.8 mi) Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

We descend through lush birch and rhododendron woods down to Phortse Thenga, then make the short uphill climb back onto the ridgeline of Mong La Pass (3,975m). After a tea break with Ama Dablam behind us, we rejoin the main Khumbu trail at Sanasa and take the wide, easy contour trail wrapping around the mountainside directly back into the vibrant amphitheater of Namche Bazaar. Enjoy hot showers, artisan coffee, and celebration in the local bakeries.

  • Key Sights: Mong La Pass ridge, relaxing evening in Namche bakeries & coffee shops
  • Overnight: Comfortable Alpine Lodge in Namche Bazaar
  • Meals Provided: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner
11

Trek Namche Bazaar to Phakding, Toktok & Lukla (2,840m)

2,840m (Lukla) 6.5–7 hrs 19 km (11.8 mi) Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

Our final day on the trekking trail. We descend the steep switchbacks from Namche to the Hillary Suspension Bridge and exit Sagarmatha National Park at Monjo. We stop at Phakding or Toktok for lunch along the Dudh Koshi River, then make the gentle final uphill climb back to Lukla. In the evening, we host a special celebratory farewell dinner with our Sherpa guides and porters, sharing stories, music, and appreciation for a successful expedition.

  • Key Sights: Final suspension bridge crossings, celebratory Sherpa farewell party
  • Overnight: Comfortable Teahouse Lodge in Lukla
  • Meals Provided: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner (Special Farewell Celebration)
12

Morning Mountain Flight Lukla to Kathmandu (1,400m) & Hotel Check-In

1,400m (Kathmandu) 35 min flight Scenic Twin-Otter Flight Breakfast

Early morning takeoff from Lukla for your scenic return flight to Kathmandu (or Ramechhap during peak flight redirection seasons, followed by private air-conditioned vehicle transfer to Kathmandu). Upon arriving at your 4-star hotel in Thamel, relax with a hot bath, rejuvenate your body with an Ayurvedic massage, or stroll through the historic cobblestone alleys of the tourist hub.

  • Key Sights: Aerial views of Himalayan foothill valleys, private transfer to 4★ hotel
  • Overnight: 4-Star Hotel in Kathmandu (e.g., Aloft / Mulberry / Fairfield by Marriott)
  • Meals Provided: Breakfast
13

Weather Buffer Day & Kathmandu UNESCO World Heritage Cultural Tour

1,400m (Kathmandu) Full Day Buffer UNESCO Heritage Tour Breakfast & Farewell Dinner

A critical buffer day built into our itinerary to safeguard against any Lukla mountain flight weather delays. If flights operate on schedule, enjoy a guided sightseeing tour of Kathmandu's finest UNESCO World Heritage Sites: the sacred Hindu temple complex of Pashupatinath, the ancient Buddhist stupa of Boudhanath, and the hilltop Monkey Temple (Swayambhunath). In the evening, we gather for a grand farewell cultural dinner with live traditional Nepali music and dance.

  • Key Sights: Pashupatinath Temple, Boudhanath Stupa, Swayambhunath, authentic cultural dinner
  • Overnight: 4-Star Hotel in Kathmandu
  • Meals Provided: Breakfast, Farewell Cultural Dinner
14

Final International Departure from Kathmandu (TIA)

Tribhuvan Airport Transfer 3 hrs prior International Flight Breakfast

Enjoy a leisurely breakfast at your hotel and do any final souvenir shopping in Thamel. Our representative will meet you at the hotel lobby and provide a private air-conditioned vehicle transfer to Tribhuvan International Airport (TIA) approximately 3 hours prior to your scheduled international flight departure, bringing your epic Himalayan journey to a close.

  • Activity: Private airport transfer in private car/van
  • Meals Provided: Breakfast

1. Accommodation & Teahouse Lodges on the Gokyo Trail

On the trail, you will lodge in authentic alpine teahouses run by local Sherpa families. Rooms are private twin-share bedrooms with wooden beds, foam mattresses, clean bedsheets, and pillows. In Kathmandu, you will enjoy a comfortable 4-star boutique hotel with private en-suite bathrooms, heating, and buffet breakfast.

At lower elevations (Lukla, Phakding, Namche Bazaar), private en-suite bathrooms and in-room charging points are often available. Higher up the valley in Dole, Machhermo, and Gokyo (4,790m), lodges feature communal dining halls heated in the evenings with traditional central stoves, with shared Western-style or squat toilets.

💡 Sleeping Comfort: While teahouse blankets are provided, bedrooms are unheated and temperatures at Gokyo drop below -10°C at night. We supply a complimentary sanitized -20°C alpine down sleeping bag for all trekkers.
2. Available Food, Trail Nutrition & Safe Drinking Water

All three daily meals (13 Breakfasts, 11 Lunches, 11 Dinners) on the trek are included. You may order directly from the varied teahouse menus prepared fresh to order by trained kitchen cooks.

  • Breakfast: Oatmeal porridge, muesli with warm milk, Tibetan flatbread with honey/jam, eggs (fried, scrambled, omelets), pancakes, hash browns, and hot ginger lemon tea.
  • Lunch & Dinner: The legendary Dal Bhat Power 24 Hour (steamed rice, yellow lentil soup, seasonal vegetable curry, spinach, pickles, with free extra servings!), Sherpa vegetable stew (Shakpa), momo dumplings, fried noodles (Chowmein), macaroni, and garlic soup.
🥗 Senior Guide Advice: We strongly advise eating a vegetarian diet above Namche Bazaar (3,440m) as meat is portered without refrigeration. Bring reusable insulated water bottles; safe boiled water or purification tablets (Aquatabs / Katadyn) are recommended over disposable plastic bottles.
3. Kathmandu to Lukla Flight Details & Ramechhap Transit

The 35-minute twin-otter mountain flight to Tenzing-Hillary Airport in Lukla (2,840m) provides spectacular aerial views of the Himalayan range.

Peak Season Ramechhap Transit: During peak months (March–May & October–November), Civil Aviation Authority of Nepal (CAAN) redirects Lukla flights to Manthali Airport in Ramechhap to avoid Kathmandu airport air traffic congestion. We provide private tourist vehicle transfers from Kathmandu to Ramechhap (approx. 4 hours) included in your package.

🚁 Helicopter Upgrade: Trekkers wishing to bypass the Ramechhap overland drive can upgrade to a direct private or shared helicopter flight from Kathmandu to Lukla upon request.
4. Trek Difficulty, Trail Profile & Physical Fitness Preparation

The Gokyo Lakes Trek is graded Moderate to Strenuous. While no technical mountaineering gear (ropes, harnesses, crampons) is required, the trek entails 5 to 7 hours of daily walking over stone stairs, undulating ridges, and rocky moraines, reaching 5,357m at the summit of Gokyo Ri.

Recommended Training: 8 to 12 weeks of aerobic cardiovascular exercise (stair climbing, jogging, swimming, cycling) combined with weighted backpack day hikes (5–8kg) will ensure you comfortably enjoy each trekking day.

🎯 Pacing Secret: High-altitude trekking is about consistency, not speed. Maintain the Sherpa rhythm "Bistari, Bistari" (slowly, slowly) and drink 4 liters of water daily.
5. Best Seasons, Climate & Monthly Temperature Trends

The ideal trekking windows for the Gokyo Valley are Spring (March–May) and Autumn (September–November):

  • Spring (Mar–May): Mild daytime temperatures (12°C to 18°C in valleys), rhododendron blooms covering lower hillsides, and thawing cobalt lakes.
  • Autumn (Sep–Nov): The most popular season with crystal-clear post-monsoon skies, optimal mountain visibility, and crisp alpine air.
❄️ Winter Notes: December to February offers uncrowded trails and brilliant skies, but lake surfaces freeze and night temperatures at Gokyo drop to -20°C.
6. Altitude Sickness (AMS), Symptoms & Acclimatization Strategy

Acute Mountain Sickness (AMS) can impact anyone above 2,800m. Symptoms include mild headache, nausea, loss of appetite, insomnia, and fatigue.

Our Safety Protocol:

  • Engineered 2-stage rest days at Namche Bazaar (3,440m) and Dole/Machhermo (4,470m).
  • Twice-daily pulse oximeter blood oxygen saturation (SpO2) health audits conducted by your guide.
  • Medical-grade oxygen bottle and first aid medical kit on standby with your trek crew.
💊 Diamox (Acetazolamide): Carrying Diamox (125mg–250mg twice daily) is recommended upon consultation with your physician before departure.
7. Nepal Trekking Permits & Entry Clearances

Two official permits are mandatory for the Gokyo Lakes Trek:

  • Sagarmatha National Park Entry Permit: NPR 3,000 (~$25 USD) per person.
  • Khumbu Pasang Lhamu Rural Municipality Permit: NPR 2,000 (~$16 USD) per person.
✓ 100% Arranged for You: All permit fees, government taxes, and registration clearances are completely handled by Best Treks Nepal and included in your package.
8. Mandatory High-Altitude Travel & Helicopter Evacuation Insurance

Travel insurance is mandatory for all international trekkers. Your policy must explicitly cover high-altitude trekking up to 5,500 meters and include emergency medical repatriation and helicopter evacuation dispatch.

🛡️ Recommended Providers: World Nomads, Ripcord Rescue Travel Insurance, Global Rescue, and Allianz Global Assistance are reliable global providers trusted across the Himalayas.
9. Electricity, Device Charging & Mobile Network / Wi-Fi Access

Solar power and hydroelectricity are available in teahouses. Lodges charge a nominal fee ($2–$4 USD) for charging power banks, cameras, and phones in the dining room.

Cellular Coverage & Wi-Fi: Ncell and Nepal Telecom (NTC) 4G mobile signals reach Namche, Dole, and intermittently into Gokyo. Teahouses sell Everest Link satellite Wi-Fi prepaid cards (approx. $5 USD for 10GB).

🔋 Power Tip: Cold temperatures drain lithium batteries quickly. Keep phone and camera batteries inside your sleeping bag or inner jacket pockets at night.
10. Luggage Allowance, Duffel Bags & Ethical Porter Welfare

Domestic Lukla flight baggage allowance is 15 kg (33 lbs) total per trekker (10kg main duffel + 5kg daypack). We provide a heavy-duty waterproof 80L company duffel bag for your main gear.

Ethical Porter Care (IPPG Compliant): One porter is allocated for every 2 trekkers, carrying a maximum of 20 kg. All porters receive fair living wages, medical insurance, high-altitude gear, and warm shelter.

🎒 Luggage Storage: Non-trekking urban luggage, formal clothes, and extra suitcases can be stored securely for free in the luggage locker of our Kathmandu partner hotel.
11. Nepal Tourist Visa on Arrival & Passport Requirements

Tourist visas are easily obtained on arrival at Tribhuvan International Airport (TIA) in Kathmandu. Your passport must have at least 6 months validity beyond your departure date and one blank page.

  • 15 Days Visa: $30 USD
  • 30 Days Visa (Recommended for this 14-day trek): $50 USD
  • 90 Days Visa: $125 USD
💵 Payment: Visa fees can be paid in cash (USD, EUR, GBP, AUD, CAD) or via international credit card at airport electronic kiosks.
12. Trail Currency, ATM Availability & Incidental Expenses

There are no functioning ATMs above Namche Bazaar. Credit cards are not accepted in high teahouses in Dole, Machhermo, and Gokyo.

💵 Recommended Pocket Money: Withdraw or exchange approximately $200–$250 USD in Nepali Rupees (NPR) cash in Kathmandu or Namche Bazaar for personal snacks, specialty coffee, hot showers, and crew gratuities.

Teahouse Lodges, Food, Electricity & Water

Trekking through the Gokyo Valley is supported by cozy, family-run Sherpa teahouse lodges. Below is a detailed breakdown of what to expect for accommodations, dining, battery charging, hot showers, and pure drinking water.

Sherpa Teahouse Lodges

Private Twin-Share Rooms & Heated Dining Halls
You will stay in handpicked, clean Sherpa lodges. Rooms feature two wooden single beds with foam mattresses, clean bedsheets, and pillows. In Lukla, Phakding, and Namche, en-suite bathrooms are standard. Higher up in Dole, Machhermo, and Gokyo, lodges are cozy alpine refuges with clean shared Western/squat toilets.
  • Complimentary -20°C alpine down sleeping bag provided
  • Heated central dining hall warmed by evening iron stove
  • Single supplement room upgrades available on request

Hearty Mountain Menus

3 Fresh Daily Meals · Vegetarian & Vegan Friendly
All meals on the trail (Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner) are full-board and prepared fresh to order. Fuel up on legendary Dal Bhat Power 24 Hour (unlimited lentil soup, rice, and vegetable curry), Tibetan bread, porridge, vegetable momos, Sherpa potato stew (Shakpa), pasta, pizza, and fresh garlic soup.
  • Unlimited Dal Bhat refills on every lunch & dinner
  • Garlic soup served daily as an active natural altitude aid
  • Celiac, vegan, and dairy-free dietary adjustments handled

Power, Charging & Wi-Fi

Solar Hydro Power & Everest Link High-Speed Internet
Dining halls have charging stations for phones, cameras, and power banks (approx. $2–$4 USD per full charge). High-speed Everest Link Wi-Fi cards (10GB / 20GB) work in all lodges across the valley. NTC and Ncell 4G mobile networks have good signal up to Namche, Mong La, and Dole.
  • Everest Link Wi-Fi cards sold at all lodge reception desks

Hot Showers & Pure Water

Gas-Geyser Showers & Plastic-Free Drinking Water

Lodge-boiled drinking water is available for purchase, or use filtration bottles and purification tablets (Aquatabs / SteriPEN).
  • Hot gas-geyser showers available up to Gokyo 3rd Lake
  • Bring 2x 1-liter reusable Nalgene or insulated water bottles
  • Boiled water doubles as a cozy bed warmer inside your sleeping bag

Certified Wilderness Medicine & Emergency Standby

Altitude Safety, AMS Protocols & Emergency Rescue

Ascending to 5,357m (Gokyo Ri) requires disciplined acclimatization, daily physiological tracking, and rapid-response emergency protocols. Every Best Treks Nepal guide is a certified Wilderness First Responder (WFR) operating under zero-compromise safety standards.

Twice-Daily Medical Diagnostics

Clinical Pulse Oximetry & Lake Louise AMS Scoring
Every morning at breakfast and evening after dinner, your lead Sherpa conducts a non-invasive biometric check measuring your blood oxygen saturation (SpO2%) and resting pulse rate. We log daily trends against the Lake Louise Acute Mountain Sickness scoring system to identify early symptoms long before they become critical.
  • Daily SpO2% logbook maintained for every individual trekker
  • Immediate rest or descent protocols triggered if SpO2 drops below 70%
  • Continuous monitoring of headache, appetite, fatigue, and sleep quality

Oxygen & Expedition Pharmacy

Emergency Cylinders & Wilderness First Aid Kits
Your expedition carries medical oxygen cylinders equipped with non-rebreather masks for immediate high-altitude stabilization. Our comprehensive medical kit includes Diamox (Acetazolamide), Dexamethasone (for cerebral edema), Nifedipine (for pulmonary edema), analgesics, antibiotics, and sterile trauma dressings.
  • Supplemental bottled oxygen available instantly on trail and in lodge
  • Certified guides trained in high-altitude pharmaceutical administration
  • Gamow hyperbaric bag support coordinateable at regional clinic hubs

Proven Acclimatization Schedule

Climb High, Sleep Low · 2 Staged Rest Intervals
Our 14-day itinerary follows strict Himalayan mountaineering physiology: 2 full acclimatization intervals in Namche Bazaar (3,440m) with an active day-hike to Hotel Everest View (3,880m), and in Dole (4,200m). Daily sleeping altitude gains above 3,000m are strictly limited to 400–500m to allow complete red blood cell adaptation.
  • 98.6% summit success rate achieved through physiological pacing
  • Strict "Bistari, Bistari" (slow and steady) cadence dictated by Sherpas
  • Dedicated buffer day incorporated to absorb unforeseen delays

24/7 Satellite SOS & Heli Rescue

Direct Kathmandu Hospital Airlift in 90–120 Minutes
Every expedition is equipped with satellite communication (Garmin inReach / Iridium) connected directly to our 24/7 Kathmandu operations command. In critical emergencies, we trigger priority B3e/H125 helicopter evacuation to world-class hospitals (CIWEC Hospital / Era Health Center in Kathmandu) within 90 to 120 minutes of request.
  • Guaranteed helicopter landing zones mapped at all Gokyo valley villages
  • Zero-delay direct insurance coordination handled by our ground team
  • Assistant guide accompanies descending trekker to provide care

Route & Views

How does the Gokyo Lakes Trek compare with Everest Base Camp?

+

Gokyo Lakes offers vastly superior mountain panoramic viewpoints. Standing atop Gokyo Ri (5,357m), you have an unobstructed 360-degree vista of four 8,000-meter peaks (Everest, Lhotse, Makalu, and Cho Oyu), whereas Everest itself is hidden behind Nuptse from Base Camp. Gokyo also has ~50% less foot traffic, six crystalline turquoise glacial lakes, and direct walking access along the colossal Ngozumpa Glacier.

Viewpoint Verdict: For pure mountain scenery and tranquil trails without the main highway crowds, Gokyo Ri is widely recognized as the premier panoramic viewpoint in the Khumbu region.
Extension

Can I combine Gokyo Lakes with Everest Base Camp via Cho La Pass?

+

Yes! We operate a 17-Day Gokyo Lakes to EBC via Cho La Pass (5,420m) expedition. This classic high-pass traverse links the serene Gokyo Valley with the Khumbu Base Camp route across a glaciated mountain col, offering the ultimate comprehensive Everest region adventure.

Technical Note: Cho La Pass requires microspikes/crampons for crossing the summit glacier. We provide gear rentals and specialized Sherpa climbing guides for this route.
Fitness

How difficult is the Gokyo Lakes Trek and what fitness level is required?

+

The trek is rated Moderate to Challenging (Grade 4/5). Trekkers walk an average of 5 to 7 hours per day carrying a light 5–6 kg daypack over rocky trails, stone steps, and glacial moraine. Prior multi-day hiking experience is helpful but not mandatory.

Recommended Preparation: 6 to 8 weeks of pre-trip cardiovascular conditioning (stair climbing with a 5kg pack, jogging, hill walking) will make your trek thoroughly enjoyable.
Flights

What happens if Lukla flights are delayed or canceled due to weather?

+

Mountain weather can occasionally ground flights. We build a dedicated buffer day into Day 13 of your itinerary. If fixed-wing aircraft cannot fly, our operations dispatch coordinates shared emergency helicopter flights ($350–$500 per seat surcharge) to ensure you do not miss international connecting flights.

Peak Season Operations: During peak months (Oct–Nov, Apr–May), CAAN sometimes routes flights via Ramechhap Airport (Manthali). We provide comfortable private ground transfers from Kathmandu.
Luggage

What is the baggage weight limit for Lukla flights and porters?

+

The domestic flight weight limit to Lukla is strictly 15 kg (33 lbs) total per trekker (10 kg for your main duffel bag and 5 kg for your daypack). We provide 1 porter for every 2 trekkers (carrying up to 20–24 kg total) strictly following International Porter Protection Group (IPPG) humane load guidelines.

City Luggage Storage: Free secure luggage storage for your excess city clothes and suitcase is provided at your Kathmandu hotel during the trek.

En-suite western flush bathrooms are standard in Lukla, Phakding, and Namche. Higher stations (Dole, Machhermo, Gokyo) feature cozy alpine lodges with clean shared facilities.

Complimentary Down Gear: We provide a sanitized -20°C high-altitude down sleeping bag on loan for the duration of your expedition.
