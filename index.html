Got Questions? We Have Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about permits, fitness, altitude safety, booking, and trekking in Nepal.

Planning

What is the best time to trek in Nepal?

The prime trekking seasons in Nepal are Autumn (September to November) and Spring (March to May). Both offer optimal mountain weather, warm daytime temperatures, and crystal-clear Himalayan panoramas.

Spring: Wild blooming rhododendrons & green valleys. Autumn: Coldest blue skies & crisp peak visibility.
Permits

Do I need a permit to trek in Nepal?

Yes. Most treks require a TIMS Card (Trekkers' Information Management System) and a National Park or Conservation Area entry permit. Restricted zones (Upper Mustang, Dolpo, Manaslu) require special government clearance permits.

Zero Hassle: Best Treks Nepal handles 100% of your permit arrangements prior to departure — all costs are included transparently in your trip package.
Fitness

How physically fit do I need to be for high-altitude trekking?

Fitness requirements vary by difficulty grade. Moderate treks (Ghorepani Poon Hill, Mardi Himal) require regular walking fitness. Challenging routes (Everest Base Camp, Annapurna Circuit, Manaslu) require hiking 5–7 hours per day over rugged terrain.

We recommend 8–12 weeks of pre-trip conditioning: stairmaster climbing, weekend trail hikes with a loaded daypack, and cardiovascular training.

Packages

What is included in your all-inclusive trek packages?

Our standard package includes everything for a safe, stress-free expedition:

  • Certified English-speaking Lead Sherpa Guide & Porters
  • All trek permits, national park fees & TIMS cards
  • Full teahouse lodging & 3 daily meals on trail
  • Kathmandu airport transfers & domestic flights (e.g. Lukla)
  • High-altitude pulse oximeter monitoring & first aid kit
Altitude

How do you prevent and manage Altitude Sickness (AMS)?

Safety is our absolute #1 priority. Our itineraries follow strict Himalayan acclimatization rules (max 300–500m daily elevation gain above 3,000m with mandatory acclimatization rest days).

Our guides conduct daily oxygen saturation & heart rate checks using pulse oximeters. If mild symptoms appear, we pause or descend. Emergency helicopter evacuation insurance coordination is available 24/7.

Booking

How do I book a trek & what are the payment terms?

Booking takes under 2 minutes online! Select your trek, date, and team size on our Booking Page. A 20% advance deposit secures your guide and permits.

We accept Credit/Debit Cards, Wire Transfer, Wise, and PayPal. The remaining balance can be paid online or in Kathmandu upon arrival. Flexible trip date changes allowed up to 14 days prior to departure.
