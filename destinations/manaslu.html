Geographic Anatomy & Restricted Biosphere

The Premier Off-The-Beaten-Path Himalayan Circuit

Established in 1998, the Manaslu Conservation Area (MCAP) encompasses 1,663 km² in Gorkha District. Named after Mt. Manaslu (from Sanskrit Manasa, meaning "Mountain of the Spirit"), this protected region was only opened to foreign trekkers in 1991 under strict restricted-area regulations designed to preserve indigenous Buddhist culture.

The region divides into two legendary trekking valleys:

1

The Nubri Valley & Larkya Corridor

Following the dramatic Budhi Gandaki River gorge northward past Samagaon and Samdo before making the high alpine traverse across Larkya La Pass (5,106m) into Dharapani.

2

The Sacred Tsum Valley (Beyul Kyimolung)

A hidden sacred valley branching east at Lokpa. Revered as a sacred Buddhist sanctuary of complete non-violence where animal slaughter is forbidden by centuries-old decree.

Nubri & Tsumba Heritage

Ancient Buddhist Beyuls & Living Tradition

The upper Manaslu region comprises the Nubri and Tsum cultural areas. Tsum Valley was designated as a Shyagya (non-violence area) in 1920 by Buddhist masters, making hunting, slaughter, and fires strictly forbidden. The people maintain pure Tibetan dialect, traditional chuba attire, and polyandric family structures in remote villages.

Ribung Gompa (Samagaon) — An imposing 500-year-old monastery beneath Mount Manaslu.
Mu Gompa & Rachen Gompa — Ancient high monasteries in Tsum housing sacred Buddhist scriptures.

Responsible Restricted Area Etiquette

Respect non-violence in Tsum — Do not carry or request fresh meat within the sacred valley boundaries.
Always stay with your guide — Nepal immigration checkpoints strictly cross-reference guide licences with RAP permits.
Leave No Trace — Pack out all batteries and non-biodegradable waste.

Teahouse Standards & Trail Dining

Once a pure camping trek, Manaslu now offers comfortable teahouse accommodation throughout the entire circuit.

Authentic Stone Teahouses

Twin-share rooms with wooden partitions. Comfortable foam mattresses. High camp at Dharmasala features simple stone dormitories with warm shared dining.

Wholesome Trail Meals

Freshly cooked dal bhat, tsampa porridge, Tibetan bread, vegetable noodle soups (thukpa), garlic soup (great for altitude), and seasonal potato curries.

Power & Communications

Solar charging is available in all lower and mid villages for a small fee (~$2-3). Mobile connectivity is intermittent; our lead guides carry satellite communication devices.

Frequently Asked Questions: Manaslu Region

Everything you need to know about restricted area permits, minimum group requirements, Larkya La Pass safety, and booking.

Permits & Solo Rules

Can I trek the Manaslu Circuit solo without a guide?

No. Under Nepal Department of Immigration regulations, Manaslu is a restricted area requiring a minimum of two foreign trekkers and an authorized, licensed Nepal trekking guide registered through a certified agency like Best Treks Nepal. If you are traveling solo, we match you with guaranteed departure groups.

Comparison

How does Manaslu compare to the Annapurna Circuit?

Manaslu is widely considered what the Annapurna Circuit was 30 years ago — a raw, unpaved wilderness circuit with authentic Tibetan culture and far fewer trekkers (approx. 8,000 visitors/year compared to 150,000+ in Annapurna). The pass crossing (Larkya La, 5,106m) is slightly lower than Thorong La (5,416m), but the overall route is wilder and more remote.

High Pass Safety

What is the crossing of Larkya La Pass like?

Pass day starts at 3:30 AM from Dharmasala (4,460m). The climb follows a gradual glacial moraine path over 3 to 4 hours to the summit of Larkya La (5,106m), marked by thousands of prayer flags and dramatic views of Himlung Himal, Cheo Himal, and Annapurna II. The descent is steep (1,400m drop) down to Bimthang. Microspikes and trekking poles are mandatory.

Best Seasons

When is the best time to trek Manaslu and Tsum Valley?

The prime seasons are Autumn (September to November) with stable weather and crystal blue skies, and Spring (March to May) when forests are in full bloom with rhododendrons and wild orchids. Winter crossings of Larkya La are often closed due to heavy snow accumulation.

Lodges & Food

Do I need to carry a tent or camping gear?

No. The entire Manaslu Circuit and Tsum Valley are now fully teahouse-serviced. While accommodation is more rustic than Everest or Annapurna, every village has operating lodges providing beds, blankets, and hot cooked meals. We reserve teahouse rooms in advance for all our groups.

Permit Documents

What documents are needed to issue the restricted permit?

We require: (1) Scanned color copy of your passport with minimum 6 months validity, (2) Nepal entry tourist visa copy (or arranged on arrival), (3) Passport-size digital photo, and (4) Proof of comprehensive travel insurance with emergency helicopter rescue coverage. We handle the physical permit issuance at the Department of Immigration in Kathmandu.
